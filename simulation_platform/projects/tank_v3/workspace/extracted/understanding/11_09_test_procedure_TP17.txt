## Requirements
AC-01: T1 level = 0.80 m
AC-02: Valve states during STOP intervals
AC-03: Transfer resumption after START
AC-04: Inter-cycle delay after T2 drain = 8 s
AC-06: V2/V3 open during SHUT

## Behaviors
Time 20 s: START command initiates T1 fill (page 1)
Time 220 s: STOP command closes all valves (page 1)
Time 280 s: START command resumes interrupted operation (page 1)
Time 650 s: STOP command closes all valves (page 1)
Time 700 s: SHUT command initiates controlled drain with V2/V3 open (page 1)
AC-08: Simultaneous V1/V2 or V2/V3 commands are prohibited except during SHUT (page 1)

## Constraints
T1 high-level setpoint = 0.80 m (AC-01)
Inter-cycle delay between fills = 8 s (AC-04)
