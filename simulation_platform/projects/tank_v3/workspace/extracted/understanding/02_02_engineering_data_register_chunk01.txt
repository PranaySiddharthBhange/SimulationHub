## Requirements
*   Level values shall be represented internally in metres with a nominal valid range of 0.0 to 1.0 m (C37)
*   Valve commands shall be represented as Boolean open/closed commands (C38)
*   Shutdown completion shall require Tank 1 and Tank 2 to both be at or below their low-level setpoints (C34)

## Behaviors
*   START commands received while already running shall be ignored (C31)
*   STOP commands received during SHUT mode shall be ignored until shutdown is complete (C32)
*   START commands received during SHUT mode shall be ignored until shutdown is complete (C33)
*   Shutdown completion shall require Tank 1 and Tank 2 to both be at or below their low-level setpoints (C34)
