## Requirements
V1 >= 0.78 m (Tank 1 High Level Threshold)
V2 >= 0.78 m (Tank 2 Low Level Threshold)
V3 >= 0.78 m (Tank 2 Low Level Threshold)

## Behaviors
If LT-101 >= T1_High, then V1 = 1 (FILL_T1)
If LT-101 <= T1_Low, then V2 = 1 (TRANSFER_T1_T2)
If LT-102 <= T2_Low, then V3 = 1 (DRAIN_T2)
If LT-101 >= T1_High AND LT-102 >= T2_Low, then V1 = 1 AND V3 = 1 (FILL_T1 and DRAIN_T2)

## Constraints
V1 and V2 shall never be open simultaneously
V2 and V3 shall not be open simultaneously during normal automatic operation
