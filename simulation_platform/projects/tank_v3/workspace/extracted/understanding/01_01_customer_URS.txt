## Behaviors
*   URS-F-001 - START: Initiates automatic operation when the system is in its initial condition (page 1)
*   URS-F-002: V1 opens when Tank 1 reaches the specified high-level limit (page 1)
*   URS-F-003: V1 closes when Tank 1 reaches its minimum operating level (page 1)
*   URS-F-004: V2 opens after a 10 s waiting period and transfers liquid from Tank 1 to Tank 2 (page 1)
*   URS-F-005: V2 closes when Tank 1 reaches its minimum operating level (page 1)
*   URS-F-006: V3 opens after a 10 s waiting period and drains Tank 2 (page 1)
*   URS-F-007: V3 closes when Tank 2 reaches its minimum operating level (page 1)
*   URS-M-001 - STOP: Closes all three valves and suspends automatic operation until another operator command is received (page 1)
*   URS-M-002: START after STOP continues the process operation rather than force a new empty-cycle initialization (page 1)
*   URS-M-003 - SHUT: Empties both tanks by opening V2 and V3 and inhibits V1 (page 1)
*   URS-M-004: V2 and V3 close and the controller returns to the start configuration when shutdown emptying is achieved (page 1)
