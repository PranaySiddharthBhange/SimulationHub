The process contains two atmospheric liquid tanks connected by an inlet valve, a transfer valve, and a drain valve. When the operator presses START, the controller fills Tank 1 until its high-level switch is reached, waits ten seconds, transfers liquid to Tank 2 until Tank 1 reaches its low-level switch, waits ten seconds, and drains Tank 2 until its low-level switch is reached. STOP closes every valve and pauses the sequence so a later START resumes the interrupted state, while SHUT inhibits the inlet and opens the transfer and drain paths until both tanks are empty. The model shall report tank levels and valve commands for a 900-second experiment.
