## Relationships
*   Phi = integral_A B_n dA (Flux is equal to the integral of B_n dA)
*   Vm = integral_l H_n dl (Magnetic Potential is equal to the integral of H_n dl)
*   Rm = Vm/Phi (Magnetic Reluctance is equal to Magnetic Potential divided by Flux)
*   B = Phi/A (Magnetic Flux Density is equal to Flux divided by Area)
*   H_iron = B/(mu0*mu_r) (Magnetic Field Intensity (Iron) is equal to Magnetic Flux Density divided by mu0*mu_r)
*   H_air = B/mu0 (Magnetic Field Intensity (Air) is equal to Magnetic Flux Density divided by mu0)
*   R_leak = R_gap*(1-sigma)/sigma (Leakage Reluctance is equal to Gap Reluctance multiplied by (1-sigma) divided by sigma)
*   R_parallel = 1/(1/R_gap + 1/R_leak) (Parallel Reluctance is equal to the inverse of the sum of the inverses of Gap Reluctance and Leakage Reluctance)
*   Phi_core = N*I / (R_core + R_parallel) (Core Flux is equal to N*I divided by (R_core + R_parallel))
*   Induced-voltage magnitude = 2*pi*f*N*|Phi| (Induced Voltage Magnitude is equal to 2*pi*f*N*|Phi|)

## Behaviors
*   A flux tube confines magnetic flux.
*   Signed fluxes satisfy continuity at magnetic nodes.
*   Field quantities are complex phasors in quasi-static models.
*   Linear reluctance remains real in quasi-static models.
*   Leakage model is parameterized by sigma.
*   Gap and Leak components are in parallel with equal magnetic potential difference.
*   Induced voltage magnitude is proportional to frequency, number of turns, and flux magnitude.

## Constraints
