## Relationships

*   GAP-1 and LEAK-1 are in parallel with the right-leg iron branch
*   The two branches rejoin before the lower yoke
*   Measuring coil uses the opposite electric orientation to the exciting coil

## Notes
The model represents a magnetic circuit with a series arrangement of linear flux-tube reluctances. A branch with GAP-1 and LEAK-1 in parallel is introduced after the right-leg iron. The exciting coil provides the magnetic potential source N*I. The model uses complex phasor conventions for current, flux, flux density, and magnetic potential difference. Leakage reluctance is calculated using the coefficient form Phi_gap/Phi_core = 1-sigma. Reference potentials are included for both electrical and magnetic domains. Revision-sensitive values for mu_r and sigma are tracked with legacy and released values. The measuring coil is oriented opposite to the exciting coil. (page 4)
