## Entities
*   coil (type: electrical)
*   measuring coil (type: electrical)
*   air-gap (type: physical)
*   shim (type: physical)
*   core (type: physical)
*   ground (type: electrical)

## Relationships
*   coil connects to air-gap
*   measuring coil connects to air-gap
*   ground connects to coil
*   ground connects to measuring coil
*   Phi_core depends_on Phi_gap
*   Phi_gap = 0.92 * Phi_core
*   Phi_leak = 0.08 * Phi_core
*   B and H depends_on Phi_core
*   Exciting induced-voltage magnitude depends_on Phi_core
*   Measuring induced-voltage magnitude depends_on Phi_core

## Requirements
*   experiment duration: 1.0 s (hold)
*   report: N*I, Phi_core, Phi_gap, Phi_leak, B and H, Exciting induced-voltage magnitude, Measuring induced-voltage magnitude

## Behaviors
*   IF N*I increases, THEN Phi_core increases linearly
*   IF N*I increases, THEN B and H increases linearly
*   IF N*I increases, THEN Exciting induced-voltage magnitude increases linearly
*   IF N*I increases, THEN Measuring induced-voltage magnitude increases linearly

## Constraints
*   gap: 1.50 mm (nominal)
*   gap: 1.58 mm (as-built prototype comparison)

## Physical relationships
*   N*I = 0.92 * Phi_core
*   Phi_leak = 0.08 * Phi_core

## Scheduled commands
*   Apply 50 Hz quasi-static excitation with an RMS current-phasor magnitude ramp: 0 A until 0.10 s, linear ramp to 2.0 A by 0.50 s, then hold to 1.0 s

## Notes
*   The coil must be connected with the documented polarity dot orientation.
*   The measuring coil must have its recorded induced voltage negative relative to the positive air-gap flux arrow.
*   Electrical and magnetic grounds must be instantiated in the simulation model.
*   The 1.50 mm gap should be used for AV-11; the 1.58 mm shim value is for prototype comparison only.
*   A sqrt(2) mismatch occurs if 2.0 A is used as a peak rather than RMS phasor magnitude.
*   Applying total core flux to the air gap instead of useful flux overpredicts air-gap B.
*   Omitting one of the grounds can leave the corresponding potential reference undefined.
