## Behaviors
*   If controller.actuators.T5_Heater is true, then HeatB5.Q_flow = 20000 else HeatB5.Q_flow = 0;
*   If Q_up > heatPort.Q_flow then m_con = 0; else m_con = -(heatPort.Q_flow - Q_up) / (hv - hl + Xi[1]*(cp*dT_dX + dhl_dX));

## Constraints
*   Large outflow loss factor (default 1e5) for ports above liquid level
*   Small inflow factor (default 0.01) for ports above liquid level

## Physical relationships

*   der(U) = sum(H_flow) - p_ambient*der(V)
*   m_con = -(heatPort.Q_flow - Q_up) / (hv - hl + Xi[1]*(cp*dT_dX + dhl_dX))

## Control laws
