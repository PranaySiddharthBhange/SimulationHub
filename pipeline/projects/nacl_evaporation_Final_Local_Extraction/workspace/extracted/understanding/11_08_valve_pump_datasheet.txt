## Relationships
*   2/2-way solenoid/pneumatic discrete valve commands 24 VDC
*   2/2-way solenoid/pneumatic discrete valve fails to Spring return CLOSED
*   Pump discharge headers are cross-connected to the charging-vessel return manifolds
*   Pump discharge headers supplies to Charging-vessel return manifolds

## Requirements
*   Nominal opening time of 2/2-way solenoid/pneumatic discrete valve is 0.7 s
*   Nominal closing time of 2/2-way solenoid/pneumatic discrete valve is 0.5 s
*   Leakage at closed of 2/2-way solenoid/pneumatic discrete valve is <0.02% Kv
*   Permitted medium of 2/2-way solenoid/pneumatic discrete valve is Water / aqueous NaCl, 0-120 degC
*   Minimum source level permissive of LIS-701 is >0.02 m
*   Low discharge pressure alarm of PIS-901 is <0.8 bar
*   Dry-run policy of P1 and P2 is Inhibit start / trip

## Behaviors
*   If 2/2-way solenoid/pneumatic discrete valve command is 24 VDC, then the valve opens.
*   If 2/2-way solenoid/pneumatic discrete valve fails to Spring return CLOSED, then the valve remains closed.

## Constraints
*   Nominal mass flow of P1 is 0.30 kg/s
*   Nominal mass flow of P2 is 0.30 kg/s
*   Nominal differential pressure of P1 is 1.8 bar
*   Nominal differential pressure of P2 is 1.8 bar
*   Minimum source level permissive of LIS-701 is >0.02 m
*   Low discharge pressure alarm of PIS-901 is <0.8 bar
