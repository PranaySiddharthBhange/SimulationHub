**Notes:**

This document represents a complex and partially inconsistent engineering design. Key challenges include:

*   **Legacy Data:** The system relies heavily on outdated models (SRC-07, SRC-11) and documentation (SRC-05).
*   **Conflicting Information:**  Multiple sources provide conflicting information, particularly regarding cooling setpoints (B7) and routing (SRC-05, SRC-07).
*   **Unclear Ownership:** The P&ID (SRC-03) doesn't clearly identify controller ownership for all valves, creating ambiguity.
*   **Incomplete Models:** The architecture model (SRC-11) is incomplete, lacking critical junction volumes and thermal interfaces.
*   **Data Integrity:** The simulation data (SRC-13) is derived from an "effective truth," raising concerns about its accuracy and traceability.
*   **Verification:** The acceptance test (SRC-09) is a verification source, not a design authority.
