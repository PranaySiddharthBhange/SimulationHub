Physical modeling evidence grounded in 07_13_water_nacl_medium_notes: the intended physical medium remains WaterNaCl, and 06_06_design_review_minutes states StandardWater is a topology/control integration baseline only. A condensation-related relation is given as m_con = -(heatPort.Q_flow - Q_up) / (hv - hl + Xi[1]*(cp*dT_dX + dhl_dX)).
