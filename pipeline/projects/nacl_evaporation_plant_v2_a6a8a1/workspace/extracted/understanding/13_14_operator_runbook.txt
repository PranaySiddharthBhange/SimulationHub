## Relationships
* B3 feeds B4 (fill/mix to buffer)
* B4 feeds B5 (buffer to charge)
* B5 precedes B6 (simultaneous condensate finishing branch)
* B5 precedes B7 (simultaneous concentrate finishing branch)
* controller owns automated valve set
* V11 transfers from B3 (to B4)
* V11 transfers to B4 (from B3)
* V9 adds brine to B3
* V15 transfers concentrate to B7

## Requirements
* Confirm B1 contains the water-rich initial charge and B2 contains the concentrated NaCl initial charge. [current]
* Confirm manual drain valves V26 and V27 are closed and cooling-water valves V13, V17 and V29 are aligned for service. [current]
* Confirm condenser cooling-water flow indication FIS-801 is available before heating. [current]
* Verify B6 and B7 are sufficiently empty for the next batch. [current]
* do not manually override an automated valve during an acceptance run. [current]

## Behaviors
* B3 level rises during water and brine charge, then falls while V11 transfers to B4. [current]
* B3 concentration changes primarily while V9 adds brine; then remains approximately constant until discharge. [current]
* B5 temperature rises during heating, then plateaus near composition-dependent boiling temperature. [current]
* B5 concentration rises during evaporation until target is reached. [current]
* B6 level rises as condensate is produced; later falls during return pumping. [current]
* B7 level rises when V15 transfers concentrate; later falls during return pumping. [current]
