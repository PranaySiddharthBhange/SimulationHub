## Relationships
* water-NaCl medium intended hierarchy includes PartialMedium (PartialMedium -> PartialMixtureMedium -> PartialMixtureTwoPhaseMedium -> WaterNaCl)  <[page 1]>
* PartialMedium followed by PartialMixtureMedium  <[page 1]>
* PartialMixtureMedium followed by PartialMixtureTwoPhaseMedium  <[page 1]>
* PartialMixtureTwoPhaseMedium followed by WaterNaCl  <[page 1]>
* medium pressure equals p_ambient  <[page 1]>
* V equals area (V = area*level + V0)  <[page 1]>
* m equals V (m = V*medium.d)  <[page 1]>
* mXi equals m (mXi = m*medium.Xi)  <[page 1]>
* U equals m (U = m*medium.u)  <[page 1]>
* der(m) equals sum of topPorts (sum(topPorts.m_flow) + sum(ports.m_flow))  <[page 1]>
* HeatB5 Q_flow controlled by controller.actuators.T5_Heater (if true then 20000 else 0)  <[page 1]>
* K1 is physically separate from B5 evaporator model  <[page 1]>
* B5 evaporator model exports condensate through fluid port (additional fluid port)  <[page 1]>

## Requirements
* Required properties: density rho(T,p,w), enthalpy h(T,p,w), cp(T,w), saturation pressure p_sat(T,w), and viscosity eta(T,w).  <[page 1]>

## Behaviors
* If Q_up > heatPort.Q_flow then m_con = 0; else m_con = -(heatPort.Q_flow - Q_up) /(hv - hl + Xi[1]*(cp*dT_dX + dhl_dX));  <[page 1]>

## Physical relationships
* der(U) = sum(H_flow) - p_ambient*der(V);  <[page 1]>
* eta(T,w) = exp(c1*T^3 + c2*T^2 + c3*T + c4 + c5*w + c6*w^2 + c7*w^3)  <[page 1]>
