## Requirements
* REQ-SIM-001: Simulate for 150 seconds and report the mass's position, its velocity, and the clamp's engaged/released state for the whole run. [current]  <REQ-SIM-001>
* REQ-FUN-001: While the clamp is engaged (t = 0 to t = 5 s), position shall stay exactly at 0.10 m and velocity shall stay exactly at 0 m/s. [current]  <REQ-FUN-001>
* REQ-FUN-002: At t = 5 s the clamp shall release once, and the mass shall begin moving back toward the spring's natural length (position decreasing from 0.10 m, velocity going negative). [current]  <REQ-FUN-002>
* REQ-FUN-003: Given the stated mass, stiffness, and damping, the motion shall be underdamped: the mass shall overshoot past the natural length (position goes negative) before eventually settling there, rather than approaching 0 m monotonically. [current]  <REQ-FUN-003>
* REQ-FUN-004: The oscillation shall decay over time and the mass shall come to rest at the spring's natural length (position 0 m, velocity 0 m/s) well before the end of the simulated run. [current]  <REQ-FUN-004>

## Constraints
* AC-02: `position_m == 0.10` and `velocity_m_s == 0.0` at t = 4 s (absolute tolerance 0.001). [current]  <AC-02>
* AC-04: `velocity_m_s <= -0.04` at t = 6 s. [current]  <AC-04>
* AC-05: `position_m <= -0.05` becomes true at some point during the run. [current]  <AC-05>
* AC-06: `abs(velocity_m_s) <= 0.01` at t = 8.16 s. [current]  <AC-06>
* AC-07: `position_m <= 0.0` becomes true at some point before t = 8 s (checked over 5 s to 8 s). [current]  <AC-07>
* AC-08: `position_m == 0.0` and `velocity_m_s == 0.0` at t = 150 s (absolute tolerance 0.005). [current]  <AC-08>
* AC-09: `abs(position_m) <= 0.003` holds for the entire interval t = 50 s to t = 150 s. [current]  <AC-09>

## Behaviors
* While the clamp is engaged (t = 0 to t = 5 s), position shall stay exactly at 0.10 m and velocity shall stay exactly at 0 m/s. [current]  <REQ-FUN-001>
* At t = 5 s the clamp shall release once, and the mass shall begin moving back toward the spring's natural length. [current]  <REQ-FUN-002>
