Subject: DR-02 Design Review Minutes - Two-Tank Controller
Document kind: correspondence

## Entities
* URS -- document
* STOP -- operator command
* START -- operator command
* SHUT -- operator command; aliases: shut
* active delay timer -- timer
* V1 -- valve
* V2 -- valve
* V3 -- valve
* LT-101 -- level transmitter
* LT-102 -- level transmitter
* IDLE -- controller state
* T1 -- tank
    - high level: 0.78 m [current]
    - high level: 0.80 m [proposed] (supersedes/superseded: 0.78 m)
* post-transfer delay -- delay
    - delay: 12 s [approved]
* inter-cycle delay after Tank 2 low -- delay
    - delay: 8 s [approved]
* Tank 2 -- tank

## Relationships
* STOP waits for either START
* STOP waits for either SHUT
* SHUT commands open together V2 (with V3)
* SHUT commands open together V3 (with V2)
* SHUT keeps closed V1

## Requirements
* Command precedence is `SHUT > STOP > START`. [approved]  <D-01>
* STOP closes all valves and stores the interrupted state. [approved]  <D-02>
* SHUT is complete only when both LT-101 and LT-102 are at or below their low-level setpoints. [approved]  <D-06>
* After shutdown completion, all valves are closed and the sequence context is reset to IDLE. A later START begins a new fill cycle. [approved]  <D-07>
* V1 and V2 must never be commanded open together. [approved]  <D-08>
* V2 and V3 must not be commanded open together in normal auto operation; SHUT is the only permitted exception. [approved]  <D-09>
* Do not model `shut` as a power-loss or emergency-stop event.

## Behaviors
* STOP during any delay freezes the remaining delay; START resumes the remaining delay. [approved]  <D-03>
* START while the automatic sequence is already running is ignored. [approved]  <D-04>
* START and STOP received during active SHUT are ignored. [approved]  <D-05>
