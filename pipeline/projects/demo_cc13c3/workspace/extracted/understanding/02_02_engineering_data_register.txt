## Requirements
* The controller shall start the automatic fill-transfer-drain sequence upon a valid START command when the process is idle or paused. [current]  <URS-FUN-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The automatic sequence shall open inlet valve V1 to fill Tank 1. [current]  <URS-FUN-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* After Tank 1 reaches the high-level limit, the controller shall wait 10 s before opening V2. [current]  <URS-FUN-004 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* V2 shall transfer liquid from Tank 1 to Tank 2 until Tank 1 reaches its low-level limit. [current]  <URS-FUN-005 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* After Tank 1 reaches the low-level limit, the controller shall wait 10 s before opening V3. [superseded]  <URS-FUN-006 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* V3 shall drain Tank 2 until Tank 2 reaches its low-level limit. [current]  <URS-FUN-007 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* After Tank 2 reaches its low-level limit, the controller shall return to the Tank 1 filling operation after 10 s. [superseded]  <URS-FUN-008 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* A START command following STOP shall continue the sequence from the point at which STOP was accepted. [current]  <URS-MOD-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* After shutdown draining is complete, all three valves shall be closed and the controller shall be in the initial idle configuration. [current]  <URS-MOD-004 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* SHUT shall have higher command priority than STOP; STOP shall have higher priority than START. [current]  <URS-SAF-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* V1 shall never be commanded open while V2 is open. [current]  <URS-SAF-003 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* During normal automatic operation, V2 and V3 shall not be open simultaneously. [current]  <URS-SAF-004 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* During SHUT mode, V2 and V3 may be open simultaneously. [current]  <URS-SAF-005 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The post-transfer waiting time shall be 12 s. [current]  <URS-PER-005 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The inter-cycle waiting time after Tank 2 reaches low level shall be 8 s. [current]  <URS-PER-006 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The controller shall receive two analog level measurements designated LT-101 and LT-102. [current]  <URS-INT-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The controller shall receive momentary START, STOP and SHUT digital commands. [current]  <URS-INT-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The controller shall provide three discrete valve-open commands to XV-101, XV-102 and XV-103. [current]  <URS-INT-003 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* A STOP command received during a wait state shall freeze the wait timer rather than restart it. [current]  <URS-CTL-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* START commands received while already running shall be ignored. [current]  <URS-CTL-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* STOP commands received during SHUT mode shall be ignored until shutdown is complete. [current]  <URS-CTL-003 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* START commands received during SHUT mode shall be ignored until shutdown is complete. [current]  <URS-CTL-004 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* Shutdown completion shall require Tank 1 and Tank 2 to both be at or below their low-level setpoints. [current]  <URS-CTL-005 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The demonstration run shall simulate from 0 s through 900 s. [current]  <URS-TST-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* Valve commands shall be represented as Boolean open/closed commands. [current]  <URS-DAT-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>

## Constraints
* Level values shall be represented internally in metres with a nominal valid range of 0.0 to 1.0 m. [current]  <URS-DAT-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>

## Behaviors
* When Tank 1 reaches its high-level limit, V1 shall close. [current]  <URS-FUN-003 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* STOP shall close V1, V2 and V3 and place the controller in a paused condition. [current]  <URS-MOD-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* SHUT shall command V2 and V3 open to drain both tanks and shall inhibit V1. [current]  <URS-MOD-003 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* On loss of controller power, all process valves shall move to or remain in the closed position. [current]  <URS-SAF-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>

## Scheduled commands
* The demonstration run shall apply START at 20 s, STOP at 220 s, START at 280 s, STOP at 650 s and SHUT at 700 s. [current]  <URS-TST-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
