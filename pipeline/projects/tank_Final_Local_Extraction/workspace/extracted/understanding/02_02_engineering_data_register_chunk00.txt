*   XV-101 opens to fill Tank 1.

*   V2 transfers liquid from Tank 1 to Tank 2 until Tank 1 reaches its low-level limit.
*   V3 drains Tank 2 until Tank 2 reaches its low-level limit.

*   STOP closes V1, V2 and V3 and places the controller in a paused condition.
*   A START command following STOP shall continue the sequence from the point at which STOP was accepted.
*   V1 shall never be commanded open while V2 is open.
*   V2 and V3 shall not be open simultaneously.
*   The post-transfer waiting time shall be 12 s.

*   A STOP command received during a wait state shall freeze the wait timer rather than restart it.

## Behaviors

*   **Start Sequence:** If a valid START command is received and the process is idle or paused, the controller initiates the automatic fill-transfer-drain sequence.
*   **High-Level Limit Closure:** When Tank 1 reaches its high-level limit, V1 is commanded to close.
*   **Wait and Open V2:** After Tank 1 reaches its high-level limit, the controller waits for 10 seconds before opening V2.
*   **Transfer to Tank 2:** V2 transfers liquid from Tank 1 to Tank 2 until Tank 1 reaches its low-level limit.
*   **Wait and Open V3:** After Tank 1 reaches its low-level limit, the controller waits for 10 seconds before opening V3.
*   **Drain Tank 2:** V3 drains Tank 2 until Tank 2 reaches its low-level limit.
*   **Return to Fill Cycle:** After Tank 2 reaches its low-level limit, the controller waits for 8 seconds before returning to the Tank 1 filling operation.
*   **STOP Action:** When a STOP command is received while the controller is in a wait state, the wait timer is frozen.
*   **START After STOP:** If a START command is received after a STOP command, the sequence resumes from the point where STOP was accepted.
*   **SHUT Action:** When a SHUT command is received, V2 and V3 are opened to drain both tanks, and V1 is inhibited.
*   **Safety on Power Loss:** Upon loss of controller power, all process valves move to or remain in the closed position.
