## Entities

*   tank2 / T2 (Tank 2)
*   valve1 / V1 (Inlet valve to Tank 1)
*   valve2 / V2 (Transfer valve Tank 1 to Tank 2)
*   valve3 / V3 (Tank 2 drain valve)
*   PLC-101 (Sequence controller) / tankController
*   LT-101 (Level measurement)
*   LT-102 (Level measurement)

*   SHUT (Operator pushbutton)

## Relationships
*   PLC-101 contains LT-101
*   PLC-101 contains LT-102
*   valve1 / V1 supplies to tank1 / T1
*   valve2 / V2 supplies to tank1 / T1 and receives from tank2 / T2
*   valve3 / V3 supplies to tank2 / T2
*   tank1 / T1 depends_on valve1 / V1
*   tank2 / T2 depends_on valve2 / V2
*   tank2 / T2 depends_on valve3 / V3
*   PLC-101 controls valve1 / V1
*   PLC-101 controls valve2 / V2
*   PLC-101 controls valve3 / V3
*   PLC-101 triggers URS-F-001 (START)
*   PLC-101 triggers URS-M-001 (STOP)
*   PLC-101 triggers URS-M-003 (SHUT)
*   PLC-101 regulates tank1 / T1 level via valve1 / V1
*   PLC-101 regulates tank2 / T2 level via valve2 / V2
*   PLC-101 regulates tank2 / T2 level via valve3 / V3

## Requirements
*   URS-F-001 - START shall initiate automatic operation when the system is in its initial condition.
*   URS-F-002 - Normal operation shall fill Tank 1 by opening V1.
*   URS-F-003 - V1 shall close when Tank 1 reaches the specified high-level limit. (Tank 1 high limit = 0.78 m)
*   URS-F-004 - After a 10 s waiting period, V2 shall open and transfer liquid from Tank 1 to Tank 2. (Wait after T1 high = 10 s)
*   URS-F-005 - V2 shall close when Tank 1 reaches its minimum operating level. (Tank 1 low limit = 0.05 m)
*   URS-F-006 - After a 10 s waiting period, V3 shall open and drain Tank 2. (Wait after T1 low = 10 s)
*   URS-F-007 - V3 shall close when Tank 2 reaches its minimum operating level. (Tank 2 low limit = 0.05 m)
*   URS-F-008 - The automatic process shall continue cyclically unless stopped or shut down.
*   URS-M-001 - STOP shall close all three valves and suspend automatic operation until another operator command is received.
*   URS-M-002 - START after STOP shall continue the process operation rather than force a new empty-cycle initialization.
*   URS-M-003 - SHUT shall empty both tanks by opening V2 and V3 and shall inhibit V1.
*   URS-M-004 - When shutdown emptying is achieved, V2 and V3 shall close and the controller shall return to the start configuration.
*   Experiment duration: 10 s (Wait after T1 high) + 10 s (Wait after T1 low) + 10 s (Wait after T2 low) = 30 s (This is implied, not explicitly stated)

## Behaviors
*   **Phase 1:** PLC-101 opens valve1 / V1 when START is received.
*   **Phase 2:** PLC-101 waits for 10 s after Tank 1 reaches its high-level limit (0.78 m).
*   **Phase 3:** PLC-101 opens valve2 / V2 and transfers liquid from Tank 1 to Tank 2.
*   **Phase 4:** PLC-101 waits for 10 s after Tank 1 reaches its low-level limit (0.05 m).
*   **Phase 5:** PLC-101 opens valve3 / V3 and drains Tank 2.
*   **Phase 6:** PLC-101 waits for 10 s after Tank 2 reaches its low-level limit (0.05 m).
*   **Phase 7:** PLC-101 closes valve3 / V3.
*   **Phase 8:** PLC-101 closes valve1 / V1 and valve2 / V2.
*   **Phase 9:** PLC-101 returns to the start configuration.

## Constraints
*   Tank 1 initial level = 0.05 m
*   Tank 2 initial level = 0.05 m
*   Tank 1 high limit = 0.78 m
*   Tank 1 low limit = 0.05 m
*   Tank 2 low limit = 0.05 m
