## Entities
* PLC-101
* V1
* V2
* V3
* T1
* T2

## Relationships
* PLC-101 controls V1
* PLC-101 controls V2
* PLC-101 controls V3
* T1 is initially at 0.05 m
* T2 is initially at 0.05 m
* V1 is commanded open by PLC-101 during SHUT behavior
* V2 is commanded open by PLC-101 during SHUT behavior
* V3 is commanded open by PLC-101 during SHUT behavior
* V1 is commanded closed by PLC-101 during STOP behavior
* V2 is commanded closed by PLC-101 during STOP behavior
* V3 is commanded closed by PLC-101 during STOP behavior

## Requirements
* T1 shall reach 0.80 m before the first transfer begins (AC-01)
* All valves shall be closed during STOP intervals (AC-02, AC-05)
* The START at 280 s shall resume the interrupted transfer (AC-03)
* The controller shall automatically begin another fill cycle after an 8 s inter-cycle delay (AC-04)
* All valves shall be closed during STOP intervals from 650 s until SHUT at 700 s (AC-05)
* V1 shall remain closed and V2/V3 shall be commanded open together for controlled draining at 700 s (AC-06)
* The controller shall return to IDLE with all valves closed and remain there through 900 s (AC-07)
* Normal automatic operation shall never command V1 and V2 simultaneously, nor V2 and V3 simultaneously (AC-08)
* Simulation duration: 900 s

## Behaviors
* At 20 s, PLC-101 issues START command, initiating T1 fill.
* At 220 s, PLC-101 issues STOP command, closing all valves and holding sequence context.
* At 280 s, PLC-101 issues START command, resuming the interrupted transfer.
* At 650 s, PLC-101 issues STOP command, closing all valves and holding sequence context.
* At 700 s, PLC-101 issues SHUT command, inhibiting V1 and commanding V2 and V3 open until both tanks are at low level.
* Following completion of the first T2 drain, the controller automatically begins another fill cycle after an 8 s inter-cycle delay.
* At 900 s, PLC-101 returns to IDLE with all valves closed.

## Constraints
* T1 initial level: 0.05 m
* T2 initial level: 0.05 m
* Effective high-level setpoint for T1: 0.80 m

## Physical relationships
* None

## Scheduled commands
* At 20 s: START (PLC-101)
* At 220 s: STOP (PLC-101)
* At 280 s: START (PLC-101)
* At 650 s: STOP (PLC-101)
* At 700 s: SHUT (PLC-101)
* At 900 s: IDLE (PLC-101)
