Canonical entities consolidated from the notes: PLC-101 / tankController / Controller is the sequence controller. Tank 1 is tank1 / T1 / TK-101. Tank 2 is tank2 / T2 / TK-102. Inlet valve to Tank 1 is valve1 / V1 / XV-101. Transfer valve from Tank 1 to Tank 2 is valve2 / V2 / XV-102. Drain valve from Tank 2 to drain is valve3 / V3 / XV-103. Level measurements are LT-101 for Tank 1 and LT-102 for Tank 2; chunk01 also identifies AI-101 as the analog input receiving LT-101 and AI-102 as the analog input receiving LT-102. Operator commands are START / PB-START / DI-101, STOP / PB-STOP / DI-102, and SHUT / PB-SHUT / DI-103. Boundary source SRC-101 supplies XV-101, and boundary drain DRN-101 receives from XV-103. DO-101, DO-102, and DO-103 are the discrete outputs that command XV-101, XV-102, and XV-103 respectively.

Topology grounded in the notes: 04_11_partial_legacy_architecture.txt gives the clearest material path: SRC-101 -> V1/XV-101 -> T1/TK-101 -> V2/XV-102 -> T2/TK-102 -> V3/XV-103 -> DRN-101, medium liquid. LT-101 monitors Tank 1 level and LT-102 monitors Tank 2 level. PLC-101 controls V1, V2, and V3 through output commands. PLC-101 receives two analog level measurements designated LT-101 and LT-102 and receives momentary START, STOP, and SHUT digital commands. 10_08_valve_datasheet.txt states the valves use 24 VDC spring-return actuators and are liquid isolation valves; 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk00.txt notes spring-return closed actuators, which supports the power-loss behavior below.

Automatic sequence behavior explicitly supported across the notes: START initiates automatic fill-transfer-drain operation when the system is in its initial condition or when paused. In normal automatic operation, the controller opens V1 to fill Tank 1. When Tank 1 reaches the high-level limit, V1 closes. After a waiting period, the controller opens V2 to transfer liquid from Tank 1 to Tank 2. V2 closes when Tank 1 reaches its low-level limit. After another waiting period, the controller opens V3 to drain Tank 2. V3 closes when Tank 2 reaches its low-level limit. After an inter-cycle waiting period, the controller returns to the Tank 1 filling operation and continues cyclically unless STOP or SHUT is received.

State model grounded by 05_04_control_logic_design_notes.txt: IDLE, FILL_T1, WAIT_AFTER_FILL, TRANSFER_T1_T2, WAIT_AFTER_TRANSFER, DRAIN_T2, WAIT_AFTER_DRAIN, PAUSED, and SHUTDOWN. During FILL_T1, V1/XV-101 is open and V2/V3 are closed. During TRANSFER_T1_T2, V2/XV-102 is open and V1/V3 are closed. During DRAIN_T2, V3/XV-103 is open and V1/V2 are closed. During WAIT_AFTER_FILL, WAIT_AFTER_TRANSFER, WAIT_AFTER_DRAIN, and IDLE, all three valves are closed per the design notes. The design notes also state all three valves are closed during SHUTDOWN, but this conflicts with multiple other notes that state SHUT opens V2 and V3 and inhibits V1; that conflict remains open and is captured in clarifications.

Discrete transitions supported by the notes: START edge from IDLE or PAUSED starts or resumes operation. When LT-101 reaches or exceeds the Tank 1 high threshold, the sequence leaves FILL_T1 and enters WAIT_AFTER_FILL; equality is included per 05_04_control_logic_design_notes.txt. After the wait after Tank 1 high expires, the next state is TRANSFER_T1_T2 and V2 opens. When LT-101 reaches or falls below the Tank 1 low threshold during transfer, the sequence leaves TRANSFER_T1_T2 and enters WAIT_AFTER_TRANSFER; equality is included. After the wait after Tank 1 low expires, the next state is DRAIN_T2 and V3 opens. When LT-102 reaches or falls below the Tank 2 low threshold during drain, the sequence leaves DRAIN_T2 and enters WAIT_AFTER_DRAIN; equality is included. After the inter-cycle wait expires, the next state returns to FILL_T1.

STOP behavior: URS-M-001 in 01_01_customer_URS.txt and URS-MOD-001 in 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk00.txt state STOP closes V1, V2, and V3 and suspends automatic operation / places the controller in a paused condition. URS-MOD-002 and 01_01 URS state START after STOP continues from the point where STOP was accepted rather than reinitializing an empty cycle. 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk00.txt and 05_04_control_logic_design_notes.txt add that if STOP is received during a wait state, the remaining wait time is frozen rather than restarted, and START resumes with the retained timer. 05_04 also states START while already running is ignored. 06_06_design_review_minutes.txt adds that if START or STOP is received during active SHUT, START or STOP is ignored.

SHUT behavior: 01_01_customer_URS.txt URS-M-003 states SHUT shall empty both tanks by opening V2 and V3 and shall inhibit V1. 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk00.txt URS-MOD-003 repeats: SHUT commands V2 and V3 open to drain both tanks and inhibits V1. 11_09_test_procedure_TP17.txt AC-06 requires that at 700 s V1 remain closed and V2/V3 be commanded open together for controlled draining. 06_06_design_review_minutes.txt requires shutdown completion when LT-101 and LT-102 are both at or below their low-level setpoints, after which all valves are closed and the sequence context reset to IDLE. 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk01.txt states shutdown completion requires Tank 1 and Tank 2 both at or below their low-level setpoints. This completion criterion is consistent with URS-M-004 in 01_01_customer_URS.txt: when shutdown emptying is achieved, V2 and V3 close and the controller returns to the start configuration. 05_04_control_logic_design_notes.txt also states V2/V3 simultaneous-open restriction is waived in SHUTDOWN, consistent with SHUT opening both together. However, its behavior line "IF SHUT occurs THEN transition to SHUTDOWN, close all valves" conflicts with the SHUT drain behavior and is treated as conflicting evidence.

Interlocks and command exclusions: 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk00.txt states V1 shall never be commanded open while V2 is open, and V2 and V3 shall not be open simultaneously. 05_04_control_logic_design_notes.txt and 06_06_design_review_minutes.txt refine applicability: V1 and V2 shall never be open simultaneously; V2 and V3 shall not be open simultaneously during normal automatic operation, and the simultaneous-open restriction for V2/V3 is waived in SHUTDOWN / SHUT is the only permitted exception. 11_09_test_procedure_TP17.txt AC-08 repeats that normal automatic operation shall never command V1 and V2 simultaneously, nor V2 and V3 simultaneously. Derived from the topology and sequence, opening V1 and V2 together would simultaneously fill Tank 1 and transfer from Tank 1, but the notes already explicitly prohibit this; no further control law is added.

Thresholds and timings with conflicts preserved: Tank 1 initial level is 0.05 m and Tank 2 initial level is 0.05 m per 01_01_customer_URS.txt, 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk01.txt, and 11_09_test_procedure_TP17.txt. Tank 1 low limit is 0.05 m and Tank 2 low limit is 0.05 m in multiple notes. Tank 1 high limit is conflicted: 01_01_customer_URS.txt, 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk00.txt constraint, 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk02.txt, and 09_07_legacy_tank_demo.txt use 0.78 m, while 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk00.txt URS-PER-002, 08_05_controls_email_thread.txt requirement, and 11_09_test_procedure_TP17.txt AC-01 / effective constraint use 0.80 m. Wait after Tank 1 high is consistently 10 s across the notes. Wait after Tank 1 low is conflicted between 10 s in 01_01_customer_URS.txt and 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk00.txt URS-FUN-006, and 12 s in 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk00.txt URS-PER-005, 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk02.txt page 12 note, and 08_05_controls_email_thread.txt. Wait after Tank 2 low is conflicted between 10 s in 01_01_customer_URS.txt and 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk00.txt URS-FUN-008, and 8 s in 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk00.txt URS-PER-006, 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk02.txt page 14 note, 07_12_operator_shift_notes.txt (~8 s after low), 08_05_controls_email_thread.txt, and 11_09_test_procedure_TP17.txt AC-04. Because the later notes explicitly call the 8 s value the inter-cycle delay used by the demonstration/test, that is the best-supported candidate for the 900 s demo, but the conflict is material and kept open.

Continuous physical relationships: Only 09_07_legacy_tank_demo.txt provides explicit equations and parameters. With A1 = 1.20 m2, A2 = 1.40 m2, qFill = 0.0060 m3/s, qTransfer = 0.0045 m3/s, qDrain = 0.0050 m3/s, h0 = 0.05 m, h1High = 0.78 m, h1Low = 0.05 m, h2Low = 0.05 m, the level dynamics are der(tank1Level) = (if valve1 then qFill else 0)/A1 - (if valve2 then qTransfer else 0)/A1 and der(tank2Level) = (if valve2 then qTransfer else 0)/A2 - (if valve3 then qDrain else 0)/A2. Derived unit check: m3/s divided by m2 gives m/s, so both derivatives are dimensionally consistent with level rate. These equations belong to the simplified legacy demonstration logic, not necessarily the authoritative URS, because 09_07 explicitly says it is a simplified demonstration model and does not include SHUT handling. 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk02.txt independently lists nominal inlet, transfer, and drain flows equal to 0.006, 0.0045, and 0.005 m3/s, supporting those flow magnitudes as nominal. No other governing conservation law or geometry is directly stated.

Simulation and test-harness evidence: 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk01.txt states the demonstration run shall simulate from 0 s through 900 s and shall apply START at 20 s, STOP at 220 s, START at 280 s, STOP at 650 s, and SHUT at 700 s; it also requires verification of exact stop time and exact event times. 11_09_test_procedure_TP17.txt repeats simulation duration 900 s and the same command schedule, plus expected behaviors: START at 280 s shall resume the interrupted transfer; after the first Tank 2 drain, another fill cycle begins automatically after an 8 s inter-cycle delay; from 650 s until SHUT at 700 s all valves remain closed; at 700 s SHUT inhibits V1 and opens V2/V3; by 900 s the controller is in IDLE with all valves closed. 05_04_control_logic_design_notes.txt and several notes require reporting tank levels and valve commands for the 900-second experiment. 12_10_demo_run_900s_chunk00.txt and chunk05 identify reported variables tank1_level_m, tank2_level_m, valve1_open, valve2_open, valve3_open, and controller_state. chunk06 states tank level units are m; valve open variables are numeric with units %, and controller_state is a string. The demo-run chunks are internally inconsistent and fragmentary; they are not treated as acceptance evidence except for the existence of reported variables.

Acceptance and observed behavior conflicts: 11_09_test_procedure_TP17.txt AC-01 requires T1 to reach 0.80 m before first transfer begins, which aligns with the 0.80 m test setting rather than the older 0.78 m URS value. 07_12_operator_shift_notes.txt observed at 700 s that XV-102 opens and XV-101 opens during SHUT, described as drain and transfer. This conflicts with URS-M-003, URS-MOD-003, and TP17 AC-06, all of which require V1 inhibited/closed and V2 plus V3 open during SHUT. Because the operator note is an observation note and conflicts with explicit requirement/test statements, it is treated as observed conflicting evidence rather than the final commanded behavior. 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk01.txt behavior lines for DI-103 causing all three outputs to close and chunk01 lines tying low-level conditions to closing DO-101/DO-102 also conflict with the broader sequence description and topology; those lines are retained as conflicting evidence, not the resolved behavior.

Provenance and exclusions: 02_02_engineering_data_register_chunk03.txt appears to describe legacy or partial architecture labels V18, V23, and V22 with unspecified acceptance criteria and no secure mapping to the canonical tank system valves; those entities are not merged with V1/V2/V3 because the evidence does not support identity. 06_06_design_review_minutes.txt lists T1 as a temperature sensor alias Tank 1 Temperature, which conflicts with the consistent use of T1 as Tank 1 elsewhere; this appears to be a note error and is not merged into the tank identity. 12_10 demo-run chunks contain partial values such as a time 420 snapshot and fragments around times 161-166, but the notes do not establish them as formal acceptance checks, so they are not used as checks.

Overall resolved brief for later modeling stages: model a two-tank sequential batch-like liquid process with PLC-101 controlling three on-off valves along the path source -> Tank 1 -> Tank 2 -> drain. Use LT-101 and LT-102 level measurements, START/STOP/SHUT command inputs, and the discrete state structure IDLE, FILL_T1, WAIT_AFTER_FILL, TRANSFER_T1_T2, WAIT_AFTER_TRANSFER, DRAIN_T2, WAIT_AFTER_DRAIN, PAUSED, SHUTDOWN. Preserve STOP pause/resume semantics including frozen wait timers and resume-from-context behavior. Preserve SHUT semantics as a higher-priority draining mode that inhibits V1 and opens V2 and V3 until both tanks are at or below low level, then closes valves and returns to IDLE/start configuration, while recording the conflict from one design note that says SHUT closes all valves immediately. Preserve parameter conflicts for Tank 1 high setpoint, post-transfer wait, and inter-cycle wait as open questions, while noting that TP17 and related demo notes favor 0.80 m high setpoint and 8 s inter-cycle wait for the 900 s demonstration. If the simplified legacy continuous model is needed, use its explicit equations and nominal parameters only as legacy-demo evidence, not as undisputed plant physics.
