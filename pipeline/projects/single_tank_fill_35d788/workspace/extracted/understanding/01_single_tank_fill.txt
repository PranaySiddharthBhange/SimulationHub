Subject: Single Tank Fill — One Valve, One Threshold
Document kind: requirement specification

## Entities
* Tank -- tank
    - cross-sectional area: 0.5 m^2 [current]
    - height: 1.0 m [current]
    - level: 0 m [current]
* Inlet valve -- valve
    - inflow: 0.01 m^3/s [nominal]
    - inflow: 10 L/s [nominal]
* Fill target -- setpoint
    - level: 0.80 m [current]
    - time: 40 s [expected]
* single supply -- supply
* tank_level_m -- reported variable
    - threshold: 0.80 [current]
    - limit: 0.85 [current]
    - final value: 0.80 [current]
    - tolerance: 0.01 m [current]
* valve_open_cmd -- command signal
    - value at time: 1 [current]
    - time: 5 s [current]
    - value at time: 0 [current]
    - time: 100 s [current]

## Relationships
* single supply fills via Inlet valve
* Inlet valve feeds Tank
* Inlet valve closes at Fill target (closes as soon as tank level reaches target and stays closed)

## Requirements
* Simulate for 100 seconds and report the tank level and the inlet valve's open/closed command for the whole run. [current]  <REQ-SIM-001>
* Starting from empty, the tank shall fill until its level reaches the 0.80 m fill target, at which point the inlet valve shall close and the level shall stop rising. [current]  <REQ-FUN-001>

## Constraints
* `tank_level_m >= 0.80` becomes true at some point during the run (ever value == 1.0). [current]  <AC-01>
* `tank_level_m <= 0.85` holds for the entire run (always value == 1.0) -- the level must not run away past the fill target. [current]  <AC-02>
* `valve_open_cmd == 1` at t = 5 s [current]  <AC-03>
* `valve_open_cmd == 0` at t = 100 s [current]  <AC-04>
* `tank_level_m == 0.80` at t = 100 s (absolute tolerance 0.01 m) [current]  <AC-05>

## Behaviors
* the valve is simply open whenever the tank level is below its fill target, and closed once the target is reached [current]
* Once closed, the valve never reopens during this run [current]

## Physical relationships
* 0.80 m * 0.5 m^2 / 0.01 m^3/s = 40 s [current]  <AC-01>
