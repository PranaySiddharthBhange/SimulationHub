## Requirements
* The laboratory plant shall concentrate a water/sodium-chloride batch using staged charging, mixing, buffering, evaporation, condensation, cooling and return pumping. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Charge water phase required action: Open V8, B1 to B3. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Charge water completion condition: LIS-301 >= 0.13 m. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Charge brine / mix required action: Open V9, B2 to B3. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Charge brine / mix completion condition: QI-302 reaches recipe target. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Buffer transfer required action: Open V11, B3 to B4. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Buffer transfer completion condition: LIS-301 < 0.01 m. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Evaporator charge completion condition: LIS-501 reaches batch level. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Evaporation required action: Enable B5 heater. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Evaporation completion condition: QIS-502 reaches evaporation target. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Condensate branch required action: Cool B6, then return condensate. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Condensate branch completion condition: Cooling complete and B6 returned. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Concentrate branch required action: Transfer B5 to B7, cool B7, return concentrate. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Concentrate branch completion condition: Cooling complete and B7 returned. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Cycle reset required action: Wait for both branches. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* The central controller shall command discrete on/off valves. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Automated valves shall fail closed. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Small junction volumes shall be used where multiple isolating pressure-drop components could otherwise leave undefined pressure, enthalpy or composition states. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* All non-horizontal pipes shall include static head in their pressure-drop relationship. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* The acceptance simulation shall demonstrate a full charge-mix-buffer-evaporate-cool-return cycle, including the controller parallel split and join after evaporation. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* It shall continue beyond 2500 s to demonstrate cycle reset. [approved]  <[page 1]>

## Constraints
* Both complete and time > 2500 s. [approved]  <[page 1]>

## Behaviors
* When B5 is idle, open V12, B4 to B5. [approved]  <[page 1]>
