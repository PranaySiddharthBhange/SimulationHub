## Requirements
* The laboratory plant shall concentrate a water/sodium-chloride batch using staged charging, mixing, buffering, evaporation, condensation, cooling and return pumping. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Charge water action: open V8 from B1 to B3 until LIS-301 >= 0.13 m. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Charge brine / mix action: open V9 from B2 to B3 until QI-302 reaches recipe target. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Buffer transfer action: open V11 from B3 to B4 until LIS-301 < 0.01 m. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Evaporator charge action: when B5 is idle, open V12 from B4 to B5 until LIS-501 reaches batch level. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Evaporation action: enable B5 heater until QIS-502 reaches evaporation target. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Condensate branch action: cool B6, then return condensate; completion is cooling complete and B6 returned. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Concentrate branch action: transfer B5 to B7, cool B7, return concentrate; completion is cooling complete and B7 returned. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Cycle reset action: wait for both branches; completion is both complete and time > 2500 s. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* The central controller shall command discrete on/off valves. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Automated valves shall fail closed. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Small junction volumes shall be used where multiple isolating pressure-drop components could otherwise leave undefined pressure, enthalpy or composition states. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* All non-horizontal pipes shall include static head in their pressure-drop relationship. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* The acceptance simulation shall demonstrate a full charge-mix-buffer-evaporate-cool-return cycle, including the controller parallel split and join after evaporation. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* The acceptance simulation shall continue beyond 2500 s to demonstrate cycle reset. [approved]  <[page 1]>

## Constraints
* Tank connections can pass above or below the liquid level. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* The preferred numerical treatment is a regularized asymmetric loss-factor formulation with hysteresis around the port elevation. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* The intended physical simulation medium is a water-NaCl mixture. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* A StandardWater configuration may be used as an integration baseline to verify topology and sequence behavior before enabling composition-dependent property functions. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* The evaporator/condenser may be represented as a combined simulation component provided the physical condenser K1 remains identifiable in the system architecture. [approved]  <[page 1]>
