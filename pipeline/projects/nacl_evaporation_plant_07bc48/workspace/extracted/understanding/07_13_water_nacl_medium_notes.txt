## Requirements
* For an incompressible formulation, the p_ambient*der(V) contribution may be omitted to avoid small artificial temperature changes. [current]  <[page 1]>
* Preferred treatment: asymmetric loss factors with a regularized quadratic flow law. [current]  <[page 1]>
* Apply hysteresis based on port diameter around level crossing. [current]  <[page 1]>
* State-machine and declarative curve-switch alternatives are historical options only. [archived]  <[page 1]>
* Required properties: density rho(T,p,w), enthalpy h(T,p,w), cp(T,w), saturation pressure p_sat(T,w), and viscosity eta(T,w). [current]  <[page 1]>

## Behaviors
* When a port is above liquid level, use a very large outflow loss factor (default 1e5) and small inflow factor (default 0.01). [current]  <[page 1]>
* HeatB5.Q_flow = if controller.actuators.T5_Heater then 20000 else 0; [current]  <[page 1]>
* if Q_up > heatPort.Q_flow then m_con = 0; else m_con = -(heatPort.Q_flow - Q_up) / (hv - hl + Xi[1]*(cp*dT_dX + dhl_dX)); end if; [current]  <[page 1]>
