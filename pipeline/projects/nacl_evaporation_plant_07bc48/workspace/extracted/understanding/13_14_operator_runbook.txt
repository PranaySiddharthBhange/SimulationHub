## Relationships

* B4 feeds B5 (buffer to charge)
* B5 undergoes heating/evaporation
* heating/evaporation branches into simultaneous condensate/concentrate finishing branches
* simultaneous condensate/concentrate finishing branches followed by return pumping
* V11 transfers from B3 (to B4)
* V9 adds brine to B3
* V15 transfers concentrate to B7

## Requirements
* Confirm B1 contains the water-rich initial charge and B2 contains the concentrated NaCl initial charge. [current]
* Confirm manual drain valves V26 and V27 are closed and cooling-water valves V13, V17 and V29 are aligned for service. [current]
* Confirm condenser cooling-water flow indication FIS-801 is available before heating. [current]
* Verify B6 and B7 are sufficiently empty for the next batch. [current]
* The controller owns the automated valve set; do not manually override an automated valve during an acceptance run. [current]
