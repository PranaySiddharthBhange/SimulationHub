## Requirements
* Confirm B1 contains the water-rich initial charge and B2 contains the concentrated NaCl initial charge. [current]
* Confirm manual drain valves V26 and V27 are closed and cooling-water valves V13, V17 and V29 are aligned for service. [current]
* Confirm condenser cooling-water flow indication FIS-801 is available before heating. [current]
* Verify B6 and B7 are sufficiently empty for the next batch. [current]
* Do not manually override an automated valve during an acceptance run. [current]
* First validate the same topology with StandardWater. [current]
* Do not resolve return-routing questions from pump location alone; check the approved routing change record. [current]
