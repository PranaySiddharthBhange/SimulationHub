## Requirements
* The plant shall prepare a mixed water/NaCl batch in B3 using liquid from B1 and B2. [current]  <REQ-FUN-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* The B3 target NaCl mass fraction shall be 0.080 kg/kg. [current]  <REQ-PER-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* B5 heating demand shall be 20,000 W when the heater actuator is enabled. [current]  <REQ-THM-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* The B5 evaporation concentration target shall be 0.180 kg/kg NaCl mass fraction. [current]  <REQ-PER-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* After evaporation completes, the sequence shall split into parallel condensate and concentrate branches. [current]  <REQ-FUN-008 [sheet Requirements]>
* The condensate branch shall cool B6 to 20 degC or below before return pumping. [current]  <REQ-FUN-009 [sheet Requirements]>
* The concentrate branch shall cool B7 to 25 degC or below before return pumping. [current]  <REQ-FUN-010 [sheet Requirements]>
* The B7/P1 return branch opens V18,V23,V22,V1,V3. [superseded]  <REQ-ROU-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* The B6/P2 return branch opens V20,V24,V25,V5,V6. [superseded]  <REQ-ROU-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* The recovered condensate stream from B6 shall be returned to B1 using P2 and the B1 return valve group. [current]  <REQ-ROU-003 [sheet Requirements]>
* The cooled concentrated stream from B7 shall be returned to B2 using P1 and the B2 return valve group. [current]  <REQ-ROU-004 [sheet Requirements]>
* All automated process valves shall be Boolean, discrete open/closed actuators. [current]  <REQ-CTL-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* All automated process valves shall fail closed on loss of actuator power. [current]  <REQ-SAF-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* The baseline integration test shall use Modelica.Media.Water.StandardWater to validate plant topology and control sequence. [current]  <REQ-MOD-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* The intended physical medium model shall represent a water-NaCl mixture and expose density, enthalpy, heat capacity, saturation pressure and viscosity as composition-dependent properties. [current]  <REQ-MOD-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* The WaterNaCl implementation shall document the known unresolved pump-start convergence issue separately from plant functional requirements. [current]  <REQ-MOD-003 [sheet Requirements]>
* Tank ports that can cross the liquid level shall use the regularized asymmetric loss-factor approach with hysteresis as the default implementation. [current]  <REQ-MOD-004 [sheet Requirements]>
* Small junction volumes shall be inserted wherever multiple pressure-drop-only valve/pipe elements can be isolated simultaneously. [current]  <REQ-MOD-005 [sheet Requirements]>
* Non-horizontal pipes shall include gravity pressure drop based on relative elevation. [current]  <REQ-MOD-006 [sheet Requirements]>
* Controller sensor inputs shall include LIS-301, QI-302, LIS-501, QIS-502, TIS-602, TIS-702 and FIS-801. [current]  <REQ-DAT-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* Controller actuator outputs shall include the 15 automated valve commands, P1, P2, B5 heater, B6 cooler and B7 cooler. [current]  <REQ-DAT-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* A complete acceptance simulation shall run through at least one parallel-branch split and rejoin and continue beyond 2500 s. [current]  <REQ-VV-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* The acceptance run shall demonstrate B3 target concentration, B5 target concentration, B6 cooling target, B7 cooling target and correct return routing. [current]  <REQ-VV-002 [sheet Requirements]>

## Behaviors
* The controller shall open V8 to transfer B1 liquid to B3 until LIS-301 is at least 0.13 m. [current]  <REQ-FUN-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* After the B1 fill portion, the controller shall open V9 and continue charging B3 until QI-302 reaches the batch concentration target. [current]  <REQ-FUN-003 [sheet Requirements]>
* V11 shall transfer the mixed batch from B3 to B4 until LIS-301 is below 0.01 m. [current]  <REQ-FUN-004 [sheet Requirements]>
* The controller shall not charge B5 unless B5 is idle, defined by LIS-501 <0.01 m and V15 closed. [current]  <REQ-FUN-005 [sheet Requirements]>
* V12 shall transfer B4 contents to B5 until LIS-501 reaches 0.18 m. [current]  <REQ-FUN-006 [sheet Requirements]>
* B5 heating shall be inhibited unless FIS-801 proves condenser cooling-water flow of at least 0.10 kg/s. [current]  <REQ-SAF-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* B5 heating/evaporation shall continue until QIS-502 reaches the evaporation concentration target. [current]  <REQ-FUN-007 [sheet Requirements]>
* The cycle shall not restart until both parallel return branches are complete and simulation/process time exceeds 2500 s. [current]  <REQ-CTL-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* P1 shall be inhibited when LIS-701 <=0.02 m. [current]  <REQ-SAF-003 [sheet Requirements]>
* P2 shall be inhibited when LIS-601 <=0.02 m. [current]  <REQ-SAF-004 [sheet Requirements]>
* The heater shall be inhibited when LIS-501 <0.05 m. [current]  <REQ-SAF-005 [sheet Requirements]>

* LIS-501 >=0.18 m causes transition from Step5 to Step6.  <[sheet State_Sequence]>
