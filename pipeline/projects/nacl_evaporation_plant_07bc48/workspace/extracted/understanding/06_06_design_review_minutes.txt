## Requirements
* Controller remains a sequential StateGraph with a parallel split after evaporation and a join before cycle reset. [approved]
* P1 and P2 require dry-run inhibit from source-tank level. [approved]
* Automated process valves are discrete and fail closed. [approved]
* K1 remains a physical condenser even if the legacy Modelica component combines evaporator and condenser equations. [approved]
* Junction volumes are mandatory where closed valve combinations could otherwise leave pressure/composition states undefined. [approved]
* StandardWater is a topology/control integration baseline only; intended physical variant remains WaterNaCl. [approved]

## Behaviors
* B5 heater permissive when LIS-501 >= 0.05 m and FIS-801 >= 0.10 kg/s. [approved]
