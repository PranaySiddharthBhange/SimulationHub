## Requirements
* Parameter values in later approved change records override conflicting values in this document. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Flux, flux density, field strength, magnetic potential difference and electric quantities are represented by complex phasors. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* The exciting coil induced voltage has positive defined electric orientation; the measuring-coil voltage has negative orientation relative to the useful air-gap flux. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* An electric ground and a magnetic ground shall both be present to establish reference potentials. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* The core is linear in this benchmark; nonlinear saturation is handled only by a separate model variant and shall not be inferred into the baseline. [approved]  <[page 1]>

## Physical relationships
* A positive RMS current-phasor magnitude ramp through the exciting coil produces a positive magnetomotive force N*I.  <[page 1]>
* Flux density B is derived from flux Phi and cross-sectional area.  <[page 1]>
* Magnetic field strength H is derived from B using the applicable permeability.  <[page 1]>
* Magnetic potential difference is H times mean flux-line length.  <[page 1]>
* The series magnetic potential drops plus the parallel air-gap/leakage branch drop balance the exciting-coil mmf.  <[page 1]>
* For the released linear elements, reluctance is real, so flux and magnetic potential difference share phasor angle. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* |U| = 2*pi*f*N*|Phi|  <[page 1]>
