## Relationships
* left leg in series with upper yoke (series linear flux-tube reluctances)
* upper yoke in series with right leg iron (series linear flux-tube reluctances)
* right leg iron in series with lower yoke (series linear flux-tube reluctances)
* right leg iron branches to parallel elements GAP-1 (branch after the right-leg iron)
* right leg iron branches to parallel elements LEAK-1 (branch after the right-leg iron)
* GAP-1 in parallel with LEAK-1
* GAP-1 rejoins before lower yoke (The two branches rejoin before the lower yoke.)
* LEAK-1 rejoins before lower yoke (The two branches rejoin before the lower yoke.)
* exciting coil provides N*I (magnetic potential source)
* ElectricGround included in exciting coil (electrical coil circuit)
* MagneticGround included in left leg (magnetic flux-tube network)

## Requirements
* Do not convert them to peak values in the SysML parameter model unless an explicit representation conversion is modeled.
* Both the electrical and magnetic domains require a reference potential.
* Include ElectricGround in the electrical coil circuit and MagneticGround in the magnetic flux-tube network.

## Physical relationships
* Phi_gap/Phi_core = 1-sigma
* R_leak = R_gap*(1-sigma)/sigma
* Rm is real and Vm is in phase with Phi.
