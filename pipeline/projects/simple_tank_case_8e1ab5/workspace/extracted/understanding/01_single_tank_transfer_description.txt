## Relationships
- Explicit: Tank 1 is connected to Tank 2 by the inlet valve, transfer valve, and drain valve.
- Implied: Controller controls the inlet valve.
- Implied: Controller controls the transfer valve.
- Implied: Controller controls the drain valve.
- Explicit: Operator issues START.
- Explicit: Operator issues STOP.
- Explicit: Operator issues SHUT.
- Explicit: High-level switch senses Tank 1 level.
- Explicit: Low-level switch senses Tank 1 level.
- Explicit: Low-level switch senses Tank 2 level.

## Requirements
- Explicit: The model shall report tank levels and valve commands for a 900-second experiment.

## Behaviors
- Explicit: When the operator presses START, the controller fills Tank 1 until its high-level switch is reached.
- Explicit: After Tank 1 high-level switch is reached, the controller waits ten seconds.
- Explicit: After the ten-second wait, the controller transfers liquid to Tank 2 until Tank 1 reaches its low-level switch.
- Explicit: After Tank 1 low-level switch is reached, the controller waits ten seconds.
- Explicit: After the second ten-second wait, the controller drains Tank 2 until its low-level switch is reached.
- Explicit: When STOP is issued, every valve closes and the sequence pauses so that a later START resumes the interrupted state.
- Explicit: When SHUT is issued, the inlet is inhibited and the transfer and drain paths are opened until both tanks are empty.
