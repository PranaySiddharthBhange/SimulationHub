## Relationships
* controlled mass-flow source supplies room (through supply duct)
* room discharges to ideal pressure boundary (through exhaust duct)
* Occupants are represented as people source
* gainSensor multiplies room sensor output (by 1/C_nominal)
* traceVolume measures room (room CO2 mass fraction)
* CO2Set is compared with gainSensor (normalized feedback)
* ACH command is converted to m_supply_physical (mass-flow magnitude using V*rho/3600)
* Modelica source command applies negative sign to m_supply_physical (negative source m_flow denotes flow from the source into the connected duct)
* m_supply_sourceCommand equals negative of m_supply_physical
* traceDuctIn is connected to ductIn
* freshAir is connected to ductIn
* ductIn is connected to volume
* people source injects into volume
* volume is connected to ductOut
* traceDuctOut is connected to ductOut
* ductOut is connected to boundary4
* traceVolume is connected to gainSensor
* gainSensor feeds PID
* CO2Set feeds PID
* PID feeds gain1
* gain1 feeds freshAir
* CAtm feeds freshAir
* NumberOfPe? feeds gain
* gain feeds people source

## Requirements
* The 100 kg/kg value is a numerical device only and must not be interpreted as a physical room concentration. [current]
* Use 1000 ppm absolute. [current]

## Constraints
* The owner compliance limit is 1000 ppm absolute room CO2. [current]
* C_room <= 1.519E-3 kg/kg during the approved design occupancy schedule. [approved]
* 0.2 <= ACH <= 6.0. [current]

## Behaviors
* AUTO_MIN | CO2 sufficiently below setpoint | 0.2 ACH lower limit | P-law requests >0.2 ACH | Common while unoccupied. [current]
* AUTO_CO2 | 0.2 < requested ACH < 6.0 | Bias + Kp*(C/Cnom-1) | Saturation or operating condition changes | Baseline occupied regulation. [current]
* AUTO_MAX | Requested ACH >= 6.0 | 6.0 ACH | Requested value falls below maximum | Not expected in baseline run. [current]
* SENSOR_FAULT | Sensor invalid >60 s | 4.0 ACH | Valid signal restored and reset logic satisfied | Raise fault indication. [current]

## Physical relationships
* C_out = 0.3*C_nominal = 4.557E-4 kg/kg. [current]
* m_peopleSource = (8.18E-6 / 100) * nPeo   kg/s [current]
* The trace-substance injection is m_peopleSource * C = nPeo * 8.18E-6 kg/s. [current]
* C_out = 0.3*C_nominal. [current]
* G_CO2 = nPeo*8.18E-6 kg/s. [current]
* m_peopleCarrier = G_CO2/100. [current]
* m_supply_physical = ACH*V*rho/3600. [current]
* m_supply_sourceCommand = -m_supply_physical. [current]
* normalized_CO2 = C_room/C_nominal. [current]

## Control laws
* Effective reference-run tuning after CR-IAQ-007 is Kp=6.0, bias=3.5 ACH, integral disabled, output limited to 0.2..6.0 ACH. [current]  <CR-IAQ-007>
