## Requirements
* Verify the released room CO2 feedback-control configuration over the approved 24-hour weekday occupancy profile using effective parameters current after CR-IAQ-007. [current]  <[page 1]>
* Initialize room concentration to the outdoor concentration and start at 00:00 with zero occupants. [current]  <[page 1]>
* Execute the occupancy schedule continuously through 24:00. Treat each schedule interval as piecewise constant. [current]  <[page 1]>
* Compute room trace concentration using the well-mixed zone balance. Evaluate controller at an internal integration step no larger than 1 s. [current]  <[page 1]>
* Log at 60 s intervals: time, occupant count, room ppm, room kg/kg, normalized feedback, ACH command, source mass-flow command, physical supply mass flow, people carrier flow, and actual CO2 generation. [current]  <[page 1]>
* Report the maximum room CO2 and its time. Confirm the result is not greater than 1000 ppm. [current]  <[page 1]>
* Confirm source command sign and physical flow sign are opposite for the outdoor-air source. [current]  <[page 1]>
* Confirm one-person people-source carrier flow is 8.18E-8 kg/s and corresponding trace injection is 8.18E-6 kg/s. [current]  <[page 1]>
* Trend contains 1441 rows including 00:00 and 24:00 endpoints. [current]  <AC-06 [page 1]>

## Constraints
* Maximum room CO2 <= 1000 ppm. [current]  <AC-01 [page 1]>
* Outdoor concentration remains 300 ppm in baseline run. [current]  <AC-02 [page 1]>
* ACH command remains within 0.2..6.0 ACH. [current]  <AC-03 [page 1]>
