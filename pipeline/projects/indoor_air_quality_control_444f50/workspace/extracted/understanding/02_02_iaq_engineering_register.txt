## Requirements
* The system shall provide outdoor air to the room through the supply-air path and discharge room air through the exhaust path. [current]  <IAQ-FUN-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The ventilation controller shall modulate fresh-air mass flow using measured room CO2 concentration as feedback. [current]  <IAQ-FUN-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The occupied-room CO2 concentration shall not exceed 1000 ppm under the design occupancy schedule. [current]  <IAQ-FUN-003 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The outdoor-air CO2 concentration used by the demonstration model shall be 300 ppm. [current]  <IAQ-FUN-004 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* CO2 generation shall be proportional to the number of occupants scheduled in the room. [current]  <IAQ-FUN-005 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The controlled room air volume shall be 100 m^3. [current]  <IAQ-PER-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The nominal air density for ventilation mass-flow conversion shall be 1.2 kg/m^3. [current]  <IAQ-PER-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* Each occupant shall contribute 8.18E-6 kg/s of CO2 at the design activity level. [current]  <IAQ-PER-003 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The peopleSource implementation shall use a trace-substance concentration parameter of 100 kg/kg and a carrier mass-flow command equal to occupant CO2 generation divided by 100. [current]  <IAQ-PER-004 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The nominal trace-substance concentration used for equation scaling shall be 1.519E-3 kg/kg. [current]  <IAQ-PER-005 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The fresh-air concentration of 300 ppm shall be represented as 0.3 times the nominal 1000 ppm mass fraction. [current]  <IAQ-PER-006 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The controller input shall be normalized such that a measured concentration of 1.519E-3 kg/kg produces a normalized value of 1.0. [current]  <IAQ-CTL-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The CO2 setpoint presented to the controller shall be 1.0 in normalized concentration units. [current]  <IAQ-CTL-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The ventilation controller output shall represent an air-change-rate command in ACH. [current]  <IAQ-CTL-003 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The ACH command shall be converted to source mass-flow magnitude using room volume, nominal air density and 3600 s/h. [current]  <IAQ-CTL-005 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The simulation source component shall use negative mass-flow sign for air entering the room because of the source-port sign convention. [current]  <IAQ-CTL-006 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The controller shall use the absolute 1000 ppm room concentration as its compliance limit. [current]  <IAQ-CTL-007 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The controller proportional gain shall be 4.0 ACH per normalized concentration error. [superseded]  <IAQ-CTL-008 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The controller proportional gain shall be 6.0 ACH per normalized concentration error with an output bias of 3.5 ACH. [current]  <IAQ-CTL-009 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* Integral action shall be disabled in the released hackathon reference run to avoid negative windup during long unoccupied periods. [current]  <IAQ-CTL-010 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The controller demonstration shall use the approved weekday occupancy schedule with a maximum of 15 occupants. [current]  <IAQ-SCH-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The number-of-people signal shall be piecewise constant between defined schedule change times. [current]  <IAQ-SCH-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The room CO2 measurement shall be represented internally as a Real-valued trace-substance mass fraction in kg/kg. [current]  <IAQ-INT-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The controller shall provide a Real-valued ACH command to the airflow conversion gain. [current]  <IAQ-INT-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The occupancy schedule shall provide an integer-valued people count to the CO2 source calculation. [current]  <IAQ-INT-003 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The fresh-air source shall receive both a mass-flow command and a trace-substance concentration parameter. [current]  <IAQ-INT-004 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The 24-hour reference simulation shall log room CO2, outdoor CO2, occupant count, ACH command and physical supply mass flow at 60 s intervals. [current]  <IAQ-VER-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* During the approved 24-hour reference run, the maximum room CO2 concentration shall be no greater than 1000 ppm. [current]  <IAQ-VER-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The conversion between ppm and kg/kg for this benchmark shall use molecular weights 44.01 kg/kmol for CO2 and 28.97 kg/kmol for dry air. [current]  <IAQ-DAT-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
* The value C={100} kg/kg in the peopleSource shall not be interpreted as the physical room concentration or a realistic gas mixture composition. [current]  <IAQ-DAT-002 [sheet Requirements_Register]>

## Constraints
* The ventilation command shall be limited to 0.2 through 6.0 ACH during automatic operation. [current]  <IAQ-CTL-004 [sheet Requirements_Register]>

## Behaviors
* If the room CO2 sensor signal is invalid for more than 60 s, the controller shall command 4.0 ACH and raise a sensor-fault indication. [current]  <IAQ-SAF-001 [sheet Requirements_Register]>
