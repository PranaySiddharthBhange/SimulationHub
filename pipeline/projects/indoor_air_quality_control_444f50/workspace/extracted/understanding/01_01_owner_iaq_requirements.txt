## Requirements
* The system shall represent a room, an outdoor-air source, an exhaust boundary, occupant CO2 generation, a room CO2 measurement, and a feedback controller that adjusts ventilation. [current]  <[page 1]>
* The room shall be represented as a single well-mixed air volume of 100 m3. [current]  <URS-IAQ-001 [page 1]>
* Outdoor air shall be supplied through a controlled airflow path and room air shall leave through an exhaust path. [current]  <URS-IAQ-002 [page 1]>
* The ventilation controller shall modulate fresh-air flow using measured room CO2 as feedback. [current]  <URS-IAQ-003 [page 1]>
* During the approved occupied schedule, room CO2 shall not exceed 1000 ppm. [approved]  <URS-IAQ-004 [page 1]>
* The reference outdoor-air CO2 concentration shall be 300 ppm. [current]  <URS-IAQ-005 [page 1]>
* CO2 generation shall be proportional to the scheduled number of occupants. [current]  <URS-IAQ-006 [page 1]>
* The design occupant CO2 generation rate shall be 8.18E-6 kg/s per person. [current]  <URS-IAQ-007 [page 1]>
* A numerical simulation shall demonstrate the CO2 feedback behavior over a representative daily occupancy schedule. [current]  <URS-IAQ-009 [page 1]>
* Concentration and airflow quantities shall be represented with explicit engineering units or unambiguous normalization definitions. [current]  <URS-IAQ-010 [page 1]>
* The model shall distinguish physical outdoor-air flow direction from any implementation-specific source sign convention. [current]  <URS-IAQ-011 [page 1]>
* Verification evidence shall record occupant count, room CO2, outdoor CO2, commanded ventilation, and physical ventilation flow. [current]  <URS-IAQ-012 [page 1]>
* Detailed controls tuning, the final occupancy schedule, and resolution of ambiguous wording may be established by later approved engineering records. [current]  <[page 1]>

## Constraints
* The released demonstration shall support a peak occupancy of at least 12 persons. [current]  <URS-IAQ-008 [page 1]>
