## Relationships
- Explicit: Tank 1 is connected to Tank 2 by the inlet valve, transfer valve, and drain valve.
- Explicit: Operator sends START to the controller.
- Explicit: Operator sends STOP to the controller.
- Explicit: Operator sends SHUT to the controller.
- Explicit: Controller fills Tank 1.
- Explicit: Controller transfers liquid from Tank 1 to Tank 2.
- Explicit: Controller drains Tank 2.
- Explicit: High-level switch senses Tank 1 level during filling.
- Explicit: Low-level switch senses Tank 1 level during transfer completion.
- Explicit: Low-level switch senses Tank 2 level during draining completion.

## Requirements
- Explicit: The model shall report tank levels and valve commands for a 900-second experiment.

## Behaviors
- Explicit: When the operator presses START, the controller fills Tank 1 until its high-level switch is reached.
- Explicit: After Tank 1 high-level switch is reached, the controller waits ten seconds.
- Explicit: After the wait, the controller transfers liquid to Tank 2 until Tank 1 reaches its low-level switch.
- Explicit: After Tank 1 reaches its low-level switch, the controller waits ten seconds.
- Explicit: After the wait, the controller drains Tank 2 until its low-level switch is reached.
- Explicit: When STOP is given, every valve closes and the sequence pauses.
- Explicit: When START is later given after STOP, the controller resumes the interrupted state.
- Explicit: When SHUT is given, the inlet is inhibited and the transfer and drain paths are opened until both tanks are empty.
