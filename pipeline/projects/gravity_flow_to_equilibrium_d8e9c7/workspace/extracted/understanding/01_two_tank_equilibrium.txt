## Relationships
* Tank A physically above Tank B
* Tank A joined by single pipe with Connecting valve (Two open tanks stand at different heights and are joined by a single pipe with an on/off valve.)
* connecting flow runs from Tank A (source while level_A > level_B)
* connecting flow runs to Tank B (destination while level_A > level_B)

## Requirements
* Simulate for 150 seconds and report both tank levels and the connecting valve's open/closed command for the whole run. [required]  <REQ-SIM-001>
* The valve shall stay closed and both levels shall stay exactly at their starting values (0.80 m and 0.00 m) for the entire closed interval, t = 0 to t = 5 s. [required]  <REQ-FUN-001>
* At t = 5 s the valve shall open and flow shall begin from Tank A into Tank B, so Tank A's level falls and Tank B's level rises from that point on. [required]  <REQ-FUN-002>
* Given equal tank areas and the stated conductance `k`, the system shall relax toward a shared equilibrium level of 0.40 m in each tank (the starting 0.80 m of liquid split evenly across two equal-area tanks) and shall stay there for the remainder of the run once reached. [required]  <REQ-FUN-003>
* `valve_open_cmd == 0` at t = 4 s. [required]  <AC-01>
* `level_A_m == 0.80` and `level_B_m == 0.00` at t = 4 s (absolute tolerance 0.001 m) [required]  <AC-02>
* `valve_open_cmd == 1` at t = 6 s [required]  <AC-03>
* `level_A_m <= 0.75` and `level_B_m >= 0.05` at t = 15 s [required]  <AC-04>
* `abs(level_A_m - 0.40) <= 0.02` and `abs(level_B_m - 0.40) <= 0.02` at t = 45 s [required]  <AC-05>
* `level_A_m == 0.40` and `level_B_m == 0.40` at t = 150 s (absolute tolerance 0.005 m) [required]  <AC-06>
* `level_A_m + level_B_m == 0.80` holds at all times (absolute tolerance 0.001 m), from t = 0 through t = 150 s [required]  <AC-07>

## Constraints
* `level_A_m >= 0` and `level_A_m <= 1.0`, and `level_B_m >= 0` and `level_B_m <= 1.0`, hold for the entire run. [required]  <AC-08>

## Behaviors
* If `level_A > level_B`, flow runs from A to B and relaxes toward zero as the difference closes. [current]

## Physical relationships
* `q = k * (level_A - level_B)` [current]
* `level_A + level_B` is constant at 0.80 m from t = 0 onward, before and
  after the valve opens. [current]
* `tau = area / (2*k) = 10 s` [current]
