## Requirements

*   The system shall provide outdoor air to the room through the supply-air path and discharge room air through the exhaust path. (IAQ-FUN-001)
*   The ventilation controller shall modulate fresh-air mass flow using measured room CO2 concentration as feedback. (IAQ-FUN-002)
*   The occupied-room CO2 concentration shall not exceed 1000 ppm under the design occupancy schedule. (IAQ-FUN-003)
*   The outdoor-air CO2 concentration used by the demonstration model shall be 300 ppm. (IAQ-FUN-004)
*   CO2 generation shall be proportional to the number of occupants scheduled in the room. (IAQ-FUN-005)
*   The controlled room air volume shall be 100 m^3. (IAQ-PER-001)
*   The nominal air density for ventilation mass-flow conversion shall be 1.2 kg/m^3. (IAQ-PER-002)
*   Each occupant shall contribute 8.18E-6 kg/s of CO2 at the design activity level. (IAQ-PER-003)
*   The controller input shall be normalized such that a measured concentration of 1.519E-3 kg/kg produces a normalized value of 1.0. (IAQ-CTL-001)
*   The CO2 setpoint presented to the controller shall be 1.0 in normalized concentration units. (IAQ-CTL-002)
*   The ventilation controller output shall represent an air-change-rate command in ACH. (IAQ-CTL-003)
*   The ventilation command shall be limited to 0.2 through 6.0 ACH during automatic operation. (IAQ-CTL-004)
*   The ACH command shall be converted to source mass-flow magnitude using room volume, nominal air density and 3600 s/h. (IAQ-CTL-005)
*   The simulation source component shall use negative mass-flow sign for air entering the room because of the source-port sign convention. (IAQ-CTL-006)
*   The controller shall use the absolute 1000 ppm room concentration as its compliance limit. (IAQ-CTL-007)
*   The controller proportional gain shall be 4.0 ACH per normalized concentration error. (IAQ-CTL-008)
*   The controller proportional gain shall be 6.0 ACH per normalized concentration error with an output bias of 3.5 ACH. (IAQ-CTL-009)
*   Integral action shall be disabled in the released hackathon reference run to avoid negative windup during long unoccupied periods. (IAQ-CTL-010)

## Behaviors

*   Ventilation controller *modulates* fresh-air mass flow *based on* Room CO2 concentration *feedback*
*   Ventilation controller *sets* air-change-rate command *in* ACH
*   Controller *normalizes* Room CO2 concentration *to* 1.0
*   Controller *sets* CO2 setpoint *to* 1.0
*   Controller *converts* ACH command *to* source mass-flow magnitude
*   Simulation source component *uses* negative mass-flow sign *for* air entering the room

## Constraints

*   Ventilation command *limited to* 0.2 through 6.0 ACH during automatic operation.

## Physical relationships

*   m_air = ACH*V*rho/3600 (where m_air is source mass-flow magnitude, ACH is air-change-rate, V is room volume, rho is nominal air density, and 3600 s/h is the conversion factor)
