## Requirements

*   The controller demonstration shall use the approved weekday occupancy schedule with a maximum of 15 occupants. (IAQ-SCH-001)
*   The number-of-people signal shall be piecewise constant between defined schedule change times. (IAQ-SCH-002)
*   The room CO2 measurement shall be represented internally as a Real-valued trace-substance mass fraction in kg/kg. (IAQ-INT-001)
*   The controller shall provide a Real-valued ACH command to the airflow conversion gain. (IAQ-INT-002)
*   The occupancy schedule shall provide an integer-valued people count to the CO2 source calculation. (IAQ-INT-003)
*   The fresh-air source shall receive both a mass-flow command and a trace-substance concentration parameter. (IAQ-INT-004)
*   If the room CO2 sensor signal is invalid for more than 60 s, the controller shall command 4.0 ACH and raise a sensor-fault indication. (IAQ-SAF-001)
*   The 24-hour reference simulation shall log room CO2, outdoor CO2, occupant count, ACH command and physical supply mass flow at 60 s intervals. (IAQ-VER-001)
*   During the approved 24-hour reference run, the maximum room CO2 concentration shall be no greater than 1000 ppm. (IAQ-VER-002)
*   The conversion between ppm and kg/kg for this benchmark shall use molecular weights 44.01 kg/kmol for CO2 and 28.97 kg/kmol for dry air. (IAQ-DAT-001)
*   The value C={100} kg/kg in the peopleSource shall not be interpreted as the physical room concentration or a realistic gas mixture composition. (IAQ-DAT-002)
