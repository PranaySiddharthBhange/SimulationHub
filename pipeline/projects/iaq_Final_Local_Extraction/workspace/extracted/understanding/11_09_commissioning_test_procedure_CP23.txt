*   Occupant generates CO2
*   Controller regulates Room CO2
*   OCC-SCH-04 drives Occupancy schedule
*   Source mass-flow command supplies Room
*   Physical supply mass flow supplies Room

*   Actual CO2 generation supplies Room
*   Trend contains data from Room, CO2, Occupant, Controller, Source mass-flow command, Physical supply mass flow, People carrier flow, and Actual CO2 generation

*   Verify room CO2 feedback-control configuration over 24-hour weekday occupancy profile.
*   Report tank levels and valve commands for a 900-second experiment (60-second intervals).
*   Maximum room CO2 <= 1000 ppm.

*   ACH command remains within 0.2..6.0 ACH.
*   Physical supply mass flow = -source mass-flow command.
*   CO2 source injection equals occupants * 8.18E-6 kg/s.
*   Trend contains 1441 rows including 00:00 and 24:00 endpoints.

## Constraints
*   Room volume = 100 m3.
*   Nominal air density = 1.2 kg/m3.
*   Outdoor CO2 = 300 ppm.
*   Controller Kp=6.0 ACH/normalized, bias=3.5 ACH, integral disabled, limits 0.2..6.0 ACH.

## Physical relationships
*   Room CO2 concentration = (Source mass-flow command * 1) / (Room volume * Air density)
*   Room CO2 generation = Occupant * 8.18E-6 kg/s

## Scheduled commands
*   At 00:00, Room CO2 initialized to outdoor concentration and zero occupants.
*   Continuously through 24:00, Occupancy schedule executed.
