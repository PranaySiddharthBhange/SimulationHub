*   `clock_time` is associated with `time_s` (time is recorded at each `time_s` interval)
*   `occupants_person` is associated with `time_s` (number of occupants is constant)
*   `outdoor_co2_ppm` is associated with `time_s` (outdoor CO2 concentration is constant)
