## Entities
*   Room
*   Outdoor Air
*   Exhaust Boundary
*   Occupant
*   CO2 Measurement
*   Ventilation Controller
*   CO2 Generation

## Relationships
*   Room contains Outdoor Air
*   Outdoor Air supplies Room
*   Room leaves through Exhaust Boundary
*   Ventilation Controller controls Outdoor Air
*   Ventilation Controller regulates Room
*   Room CO2 Measurement regulates Ventilation Controller
*   CO2 Generation triggers Ventilation Controller
*   CO2 Measurement measures Room
*   Occupant triggers CO2 Generation
*   Ventilation Controller reports CO2 Measurement

## Requirements
*   Room shall be represented as a single well-mixed air volume of 100 m3 (page 1)
*   Outdoor air shall be supplied through a controlled airflow path and room air shall leave through an exhaust path (page 1)
*   Room CO2 shall not exceed 1000 ppm during approved occupied schedule (page 1)
*   Reference outdoor-air CO2 concentration shall be 300 ppm (page 1)
*   CO2 generation shall be proportional to the scheduled number of occupants (page 1)
*   Design occupant CO2 generation rate shall be 8.18E-6 kg/s per person (page 1)
*   Demonstration shall support a peak occupancy of at least 12 persons (page 1)
*   Numerical simulation shall demonstrate CO2 feedback behavior over a representative daily occupancy schedule (page 1)
*   Concentration and airflow quantities shall be represented with explicit engineering units or unambiguous normalization definitions (page 1)
*   Model shall distinguish physical outdoor-air flow direction from any implementation-specific source sign convention (page 1)
*   Verification evidence shall record occupant count, room CO2, outdoor CO2, commanded ventilation, and physical ventilation flow (page 1)

## Behaviors
*   If Room CO2 exceeds 1000 ppm, then Ventilation Controller adjusts Outdoor Air flow.

## Constraints
*   Room volume is 100 m3
