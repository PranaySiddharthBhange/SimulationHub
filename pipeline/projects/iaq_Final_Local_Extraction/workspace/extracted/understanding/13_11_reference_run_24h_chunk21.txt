## Entities
*   `time_s`: Time in seconds
*   `clock_time`: Time of day (HH:MM:SS)
*   `occupants_person`: Number of occupants
*   `outdoor_co2_ppm`: Outdoor CO2 concentration in parts per million
*   `outdoor_co2_mass_fraction_kgkg`: Outdoor CO2 mass fraction in kilograms per kilogram
*   `room_co2_ppm`: Room CO2 concentration in parts per million
*   `room_co2_mass_fraction_kgkg`: Room CO2 mass fraction in kilograms per kilogram
*   `normalized_co2_feedback`: Normalized CO2 feedback value
*   `normalized_setpoint`: Normalized setpoint value
*   `ach_command_1_per_h`: Achieved CO2 command per hour
*   `source_m_flow_command_kg_s`: Source M flow command in kilograms per second
