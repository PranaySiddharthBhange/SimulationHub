## Entities

*   `time_s`: Time in seconds
*   `clock_time`: Time of day (HH:MM:SS)
*   `occupants_person`: Number of occupants (integer)
*   `outdoor_co2_ppm`: Outdoor CO2 concentration in parts per million (float)
*   `outdoor_co2_mass_fraction_kgkg`: Outdoor CO2 mass fraction in kilograms per kilogram (float)
*   `room_co2_ppm`: Room CO2 concentration in parts per million (float)
*   `room_co2_mass_fraction_kgkg`: Room CO2 mass fraction in kilograms per kilogram (float)
