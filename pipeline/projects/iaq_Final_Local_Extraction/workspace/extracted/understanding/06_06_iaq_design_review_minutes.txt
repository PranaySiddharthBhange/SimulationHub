## Entities
RM-201, room, CO2, outdoor, SysML, ACH, V, rho, C_source, sensor, fault indication, occupancy schedule, people schedule, trend.

## Relationships
None

## Requirements
1. Room CO2 shall not exceed 1000 ppm absolute.
2. Outdoor CO2 is 300 ppm (0.3 * 1.519E-3 kg/kg).
3. Internal controller feedback is trace-substance mass fraction in kg/kg.
4. Controller output is an ACH command.
5. Airflow conversion: m = ACH * V * rho / 3600.
6. Inflow is represented with a negative mass-flow command.
7. If CO2 sensor invalid for > 60s, fallback ventilation is 4 ACH and a fault indication is raised.

## Behaviors
1. If room CO2 > 1000 ppm, activate ventilation to achieve 1000 ppm.
2. If CO2 sensor invalid for > 60s, set ventilation to 4 ACH and raise a fault indication.
