## Relationships
* resistor in series with inductor (once the switch closes)
* inductor in series with resistor (once the switch closes)
* switch connects into loop with capacitor (The switch closes once, at a fixed time, connecting the capacitor into a loop with a resistor and an inductor in series.)
* capacitor discharges through resistor (through the loop after switch closes)
* capacitor discharges through inductor (through the loop after switch closes)

## Requirements
* Simulate for 150 seconds and report the capacitor voltage, the loop current, and the switch's open/closed state for the whole run. [current]  <REQ-SIM-001>
* While the switch is open (t = 0 to t = 5 s), capacitor voltage shall stay exactly at 10 V and current shall stay exactly at 0 A. [current]  <REQ-FUN-001>
* At t = 5 s the switch shall close once, and capacitor voltage shall begin falling from 10 V. [current]  <REQ-FUN-002>
* Given the stated R, L, C values, the response shall be underdamped: capacitor voltage shall swing negative (overshoot past 0 V) before settling, rather than decaying monotonically to zero. [current]  <REQ-FUN-003>
* The ringing shall decay over time and the capacitor voltage shall settle at 0 V well before the end of the run. [current]  <REQ-FUN-004>

## Constraints
* `switch_closed == 0` at t = 4 s. [current]  <AC-01>
* `capacitor_voltage_V == 10.0` and `loop_current_A == 0.0` at t = 4 s (absolute tolerance 0.01). [current]  <AC-02>
* `switch_closed == 1` at t = 6 s. [current]  <AC-03>
* `capacitor_voltage_V <= -5.0` becomes true at some point during the run (ever value == 1.0). Expected around t = 8.16 s, where voltage reaches about -7.29 V. [current]  <AC-04>
* `abs(loop_current_A) <= 0.05` at t = 8.16 s -- the loop momentarily stops discharging there before ringing back. [current]  <AC-05>
* `capacitor_voltage_V <= 0.0` becomes true at some point between t = 5 s and t = 8 s (ever value == 1.0). Expected around t = 6.68 s. [current]  <AC-06>
* `capacitor_voltage_V == 0.0` and `loop_current_A == 0.0` at t = 150 s (absolute tolerance 0.05). [current]  <AC-07>
* `abs(capacitor_voltage_V) <= 0.3` holds for the entire interval t = 50 s to t = 150 s. [current]  <AC-08>

## Physical relationships
* L*C*d^2(v_C)/dt^2 + R*C*d(v_C)/dt + v_C = 0 [current]
* zeta = (R/2)*sqrt(C/L) = 0.1 [current]
* omega_n = 1/sqrt(L*C) = 1 rad/s [current]
