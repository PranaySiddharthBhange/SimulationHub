## Requirements
* Flux, flux density, field strength, magnetic potential difference and electric quantities are represented by complex phasors. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Both follow the quasi-static relation |U| = 2*pi*f*N*|Phi|. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* An electric ground and a magnetic ground shall both be present to establish reference potentials. [approved]  <[page 1]>

## Constraints
* The core is linear in this benchmark; nonlinear saturation is handled only by a separate model variant and shall not be inferred into the baseline. [approved]  <[page 1]>

## Physical relationships
* A positive RMS current-phasor magnitude ramp through the exciting coil produces a positive magnetomotive force N*I. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Flux density B is derived from flux Phi and cross-sectional area. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Magnetic field strength H is derived from B using the applicable permeability. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* Magnetic potential difference is H times mean flux-line length. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* The series magnetic potential drops plus the parallel air-gap/leakage branch drop balance the exciting-coil mmf. [approved]  <[page 1]>
* For the released linear elements, reluctance is real, so flux and magnetic potential difference share phasor angle. [approved]  <[page 1]>
