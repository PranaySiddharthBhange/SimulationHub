## Relationships
* GAP-1 in parallel with LEAK-1  <[page 1]>
* GAP-1 has equal magnetic potential difference as LEAK-1  <[page 1]>
* exciting coils defined with opposite induced-voltage orientation to measuring coils (in the benchmark)  <[page 1]>

## Constraints
* Force interaction is excluded.  <[page 1]>
* The baseline benchmark does not use a nonlinear B-H curve, hysteresis, eddy-current loss, or frequency-dependent permeability. [current]  <[page 1]>
* A nonlinear inductor example may exist elsewhere but it is not authority for this case.  <[page 1]>

## Physical relationships
* Phi = integral_A B_n dA  <[page 1]>
* Vm = integral_l H_n dl  <[page 1]>
* Rm = Vm/Phi  <[page 1]>
* For constant A and l: Rm = l/(mu0*mu_r*A)  <[page 1]>
* B = Phi/A  <[page 1]>
* H_iron = B/(mu0*mu_r)  <[page 1]>
* H_air = B/mu0  <[page 1]>
* Phi_gap/Phi_core = 1-sigma  <[page 1]>
* Phi_leak/Phi_core = sigma  <[page 1]>
* R_leak = R_gap*(1-sigma)/sigma  <[page 1]>
* R_parallel = 1/(1/R_gap + 1/R_leak)  <[page 1]>
* Phi_core = N*I / (R_core + R_parallel)  <[page 1]>
* induced-voltage magnitude is 2*pi*f*N*|Phi|  <[page 1]>
