## Requirements
* The electromagnetic converter shall create positive magnetic mmf when positive exciting-coil current is applied. [current]  <REQ-FUN-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* The core magnetic circuit shall include left leg, upper yoke, right leg, lower yoke and one discrete air gap. [current]  <REQ-FUN-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* The magnetic circuit shall include a leakage flux path in parallel with the useful air-gap path. [current]  <REQ-FUN-003 [sheet Requirements]>
* The effective leakage coefficient sigma shall be 0.08 for the released benchmark configuration. [current]  <REQ-PER-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* The released effective relative permeability of the iron core shall be 1200 under the linear benchmark assumption. [current]  <REQ-PER-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* The square core cross section shall have side a = 25 mm. [current]  <REQ-GEO-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* The mean outside magnetic-circuit dimension l shall be 150 mm as defined on the reference drawing. [current]  <REQ-GEO-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* The nominal air-gap length delta shall be 1.50 mm. [current]  <REQ-GEO-003 [sheet Requirements]>
* The exciting coil shall have 600 turns. [current]  <REQ-ELC-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* The measuring coil in the air gap shall have 50 turns. [current]  <REQ-ELC-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* The exciting current RMS phasor magnitude shall ramp from 0 A to 2.0 A between 0.10 s and 0.50 s and then remain at 2.0 A through 1.0 s. [current]  <REQ-ELC-003 [sheet Requirements]>
* The quasi-static excitation frequency shall be 50 Hz. [current]  <REQ-ELC-004 [sheet Requirements]>
* Magnetic flux, flux density, field strength and magnetic potential shall be represented as quasi-static phasor quantities. [current]  <REQ-QS-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* For linear reluctance elements, reluctance shall remain a real-valued quantity and magnetic potential difference and flux shall have the same phasor angle. [current]  <REQ-QS-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* The exciting coil induced voltage shall be oriented positive with respect to the defined positive electric direction. [current]  <REQ-IND-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* The measuring coil induced voltage shall be oriented negative with respect to the air-gap flux direction shown in the reference configuration. [current]  <REQ-IND-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* The electrical network shall contain an electric ground defining zero electric potential. [current]  <REQ-GND-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* The magnetic network shall contain a magnetic ground defining zero magnetic scalar potential. [current]  <REQ-GND-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* Force interaction shall not be modeled. [current]  <REQ-ASM-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* Core reluctance shall be linear with constant effective relative permeability for this benchmark. [current]  <REQ-ASM-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* Flux sensor output shall expose the useful air-gap flux with positive sign in the arrow direction shown on the reference model. [current]  <REQ-DAT-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* Current, flux, magnetic potential and induced-voltage channels shall retain explicit units and phasor convention metadata. [current]  <REQ-DAT-002 [sheet Requirements]>

## Physical relationships
* Useful air-gap flux shall be modeled as (1-sigma) times total core flux. [current]  <REQ-FUN-004 [sheet Requirements]>
* Mean flux-line lengths shall be l-a for left leg, upper yoke and lower yoke, and l-a-delta for the right leg iron section. [current]  <REQ-GEO-004 [sheet Requirements]>
* For each linear flux-tube element, reluctance shall be Rm = length/(mu0*mu_r*A). [current]  <REQ-MAG-001 [sheet Requirements]>
* Flux density shall be B = Phi/A for constant cross-section elements. [current]  <REQ-MAG-002 [sheet Requirements]>
* Core field strength shall be H = B/(mu0*mu_r), and air-gap field strength shall be H = B/mu0. [current]  <REQ-MAG-003 [sheet Requirements]>
* Magnetic potential difference of each element shall equal H multiplied by its mean flux-line length. [current]  <REQ-MAG-004 [sheet Requirements]>
* The sum of series core magnetic potential drops plus the air-gap branch magnetic potential shall equal N*I. [current]  <REQ-MAG-005 [sheet Requirements]>
* Magnetic potential difference across GAP-1 and LEAK-1 shall be equal because the elements are connected in parallel. [current]  <REQ-MAG-006 [sheet Requirements]>
* Flux continuity shall be satisfied at the split: Phi_core = Phi_gap + Phi_leak. [current]  <REQ-MAG-007 [sheet Requirements]>
* Induced-voltage phasor magnitude for a sinusoidal flux shall satisfy |U| = 2*pi*f*N*|Phi|. [current]  <REQ-IND-003 [sheet Requirements]>
