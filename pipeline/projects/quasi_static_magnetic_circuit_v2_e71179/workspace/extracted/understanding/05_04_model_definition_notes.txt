## Relationships
* left leg in series with upper yoke
* upper yoke in series with right leg iron
* right leg iron in series with lower yoke
* GAP-1 in parallel with LEAK-1
* right leg iron branches to GAP-1 (after the right-leg iron)
* right leg iron branches to LEAK-1 (after the right-leg iron)
* GAP-1 rejoins before lower yoke
* LEAK-1 rejoins before lower yoke
* exciting coil provides magnetic potential source N*I
* ElectricGround included in exciting coil (electrical coil circuit)
* MagneticGround included in Air gap (magnetic flux-tube network)

## Requirements
* Input current values in EP-03 are RMS phasor magnitudes and are not to be converted to peak values in the SysML parameter model unless an explicit representation conversion is modeled.
* Both the electrical and magnetic domains require a reference potential.
* Include ElectricGround in the electrical coil circuit and MagneticGround in the magnetic flux-tube network.

## Physical relationships
* Phi_gap/Phi_core = 1-sigma
* R_leak = R_gap*(1-sigma)/sigma
