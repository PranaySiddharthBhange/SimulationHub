## Behaviors
* After T1 reaches low level, wait 12 s before opening the drain valve. [approved]
* After T2 reaches low, wait 8 s before the next fill starts. [approved]
* During SHUT, V2 and V3 stay commanded open until both low-level conditions are satisfied. [current]  <DR-02>
* START during SHUT is ignored. [current]  <DR-02>
* STOP is implemented as a latch that forces all valves closed; START clears it. [current]
