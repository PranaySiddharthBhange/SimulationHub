## Requirements
* START shall initiate automatic operation when the system is in its initial condition. [released_baseline]  <URS-F-001 [page 1]>
* Normal operation shall fill Tank 1 by opening V1. [released_baseline]  <URS-F-002 [page 1]>
* V1 shall close when Tank 1 reaches the specified high-level limit. [released_baseline]  <URS-F-003 [page 1]>
* After a 10 s waiting period, V2 shall open and transfer liquid from Tank 1 to Tank 2. [released_baseline]  <URS-F-004 [page 1]>
* V2 shall close when Tank 1 reaches its minimum operating level. [released_baseline]  <URS-F-005 [page 1]>
* After a 10 s waiting period, V3 shall open and drain Tank 2. [released_baseline]  <URS-F-006 [page 1]>
* V3 shall close when Tank 2 reaches its minimum operating level. [released_baseline]  <URS-F-007 [page 1]>
* The automatic process shall continue cyclically unless stopped or shut down. [released_baseline]  <URS-F-008 [page 1]>
* STOP shall close all three valves and suspend automatic operation until another operator command is received. [released_baseline]  <URS-M-001 [page 1]>
* START after STOP shall continue the process operation rather than force a new empty-cycle initialization. [released_baseline]  <URS-M-002 [page 1]>
* SHUT shall empty both tanks by opening V2 and V3 and shall inhibit V1. [released_baseline]  <URS-M-003 [page 1]>
* When shutdown emptying is achieved, V2 and V3 shall close and the controller shall return to the start configuration. [released_baseline]  <URS-M-004 [page 1]>
* Level measurements LT-101 and LT-102 shall be available to PLC-101. [released_baseline]  <[page 2]>
* The supplier shall provide actuators that fail closed on loss of power. [released_baseline]  <[page 2]>
* Sequence operation, stop/resume behavior and controlled shutdown shall be demonstrated in simulation. [released_baseline]  <[page 2]>
