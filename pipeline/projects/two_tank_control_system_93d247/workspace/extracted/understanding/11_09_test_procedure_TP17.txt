## Requirements
* T1 shall reach the effective high-level setpoint of 0.80 m before the first transfer begins. [approved]  <AC-01 [page 1]>
* All valves shall be closed throughout the STOP interval from 220 s until the START command at 280 s. [approved]  <AC-02 [page 1]>
* The START at 280 s shall resume the interrupted transfer rather than restart filling. [approved]  <AC-03 [page 1]>
* Following completion of the first T2 drain, the controller shall automatically begin another fill cycle after the effective 8 s inter-cycle delay. [approved]  <AC-04 [page 1]>
* All valves shall be closed throughout the STOP interval from 650 s until SHUT at 700 s. [approved]  <AC-05 [page 1]>
* At 700 s V1 shall remain closed and V2/V3 shall be commanded open together for controlled draining. [approved]  <AC-06 [page 1]>
* After shutdown drain completion the controller shall return to IDLE with all valves closed and shall remain there through 900 s because no subsequent START is scheduled. [approved]  <AC-07 [page 1]>
* Normal automatic operation shall never command V1 and V2 simultaneously, nor V2 and V3 simultaneously. The latter combination is permitted only in SHUTDOWN. [approved]  <AC-08 [page 1]>

## Constraints
* A tolerance of +/-2 s is acceptable for state-boundary comparisons when the effective parameter set is used. [approved]  <[page 1]>
